# CME Part 1 — Module Review

## Big Picture

CME Part 1 解决“经济环境如何形成可信输入”。重点不是准确猜每个季度，而是让长期增长、通胀、政策、利率与跨境资金流动彼此一致。

## Core Framework

决策期限 → 数据/模型质量 → 趋势供给能力 → 周期位置 → 通胀与政策反应 → 利率及汇率制度 → 资产预期输入。

## Must Remember

- 长期回报不能永远靠 P/E 或利润份额扩张；趋势增长由劳动与生产率支持。
- 市场前瞻，短端由央行主导、长端包含未来短端预期与 term premium。
- 通缩支持固定支付的实际价值，却可能破坏信用、盈利和租金。
- Taylor rule 使用题目指定的 real/nominal rate 与 gap 定义。
- 扩大历史样本要先检查 regime change；同期相关不能证明预测关系。

## Exam Integration

**Source: Original CFA-style（仅整合复习）。**

1. An economy shows an inverted yield curve and falling leading indicators. The central bank will ease; the government will issue long-term debt to fund tax cuts. **Discuss** the likely curve change and a limitation of a bond-return forecast based only on lower policy rates.

**Minimum Passing Answer:** The curve tends to steepen as short rates fall and long-term issuance supports long yields. Bond return also depends on duration, the investment horizon, reinvestment and credit spreads; lower policy rates alone do not determine total return.

2. An analyst extrapolates strong past equity returns into a long-term forecast, assuming profits and valuation multiples keep outgrowing GDP. **Identify** two problems.

**Scoring Points：** profit share cannot rise indefinitely；valuation expansion cannot compound indefinitely。短期外推还可能造成 forecasting bias，但须与题干证据对应。
