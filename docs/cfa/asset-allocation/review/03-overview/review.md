# Overview of Asset Allocation — Module Review

## Big Picture

定义投资者真正要承担的风险，建立可实施且治理清楚的配置框架，再让优化工具计算权重。

## Core Framework

Economic balance sheet → objectives / constraints → risk definition → asset classes + factor exposure → IPS / SAA approval → rebalancing / monitoring。

## Must Remember

- Human capital、受限 inflows、未来消费和 commitments 都可能改变风险容量。
- Asset-only / liability-relative / goals-based 的区别在 risk definition，而非投资者标签。
- 同类应相对同质，holdings 互斥；风险来源可跨资产重叠。
- Factor models 揭示共同风险，但不保证结果改善。
- Tax/交易成本↑通常更宽，高 risk aversion 与较低 correlation 通常更窄。

## Exam Integration

**Source: Local Other AA，Law family Case No.2025072102000041 — adapted；答案为推导。**

Two spouses work for the same firm and hold most equities in employer shares. Their near-term education goal is essential, while a donation is due much later. **Recommend** a framework and **justify** one change in exposure.

**Minimum Passing Answer:** Use goals-based sub-portfolios by horizon and required success rate. Reduce employer-share concentration because financial and human capital share the same firm risk; secure near-term education with a conservative allocation.

**Scoring Points：** goal segmentation；near-term priority；human/financial capital 共振。长期捐赠也要看成功概率，不单凭长 horizon 自动选最高 equity weight。
