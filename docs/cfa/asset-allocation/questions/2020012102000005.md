## Vignette & Exhibits

The Swiss market is fully integrated with the global investable market (GIM). Global risk premium is 3.5% and global volatility is 8.5%.

| Swiss industry | Volatility | Correlation with GIM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health Care | 12.0% | 0.7 |
| Watch | 6.0% | 0.8 |
| Consumer Products | 7.5% | 0.8 |

Estimate risk premiums using the integrated Singer–Terhaar framework. The source's second subquestion lacks valuation inputs and is not included.

### Conclusion

Health Care 3.459%；Watch 1.976%；Consumer Products 2.471%。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Global market Sharpe=3.5/8.5=0.411765。
- 各行业 RP=ρσ_i×global Sharpe。
- 分别为 0.7×12×0.411765、0.8×6×0.411765、0.8×7.5×0.411765%。

### Minimum Passing Answer

Integrated premiums: Health Care 3.46%; Watch 1.98%; Consumer Products 2.47%, using RP_i = ρ_i,G σ_i (3.5%/8.5%).

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** G–K 拆分收入、增长、股本与估值；Singer–Terhaar 根据市场整合程度区分系统风险与总风险补偿。

长期盈利输入接自 trend growth；全球整合和治理风险又影响 robust MVO 的预期与相关性假设。

**Boundary：** 整合过渡的估值增益不等于之后的均衡回报；required premium 本身不能证明资产便宜。
