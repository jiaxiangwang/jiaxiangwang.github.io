<template #prompt>

Compared to large asset owners, smaller asset owners are more likely to:

</template>

<template #a>

have smaller allocations to private assets.

</template>

<template #b>

have greater ability to negotiate lower fees with external managers.

</template>

<template #c>

adjust their portfolio slower in response to short-term market movements.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. have smaller allocations to private assets.

### Why?

较小 owners 常缺 minimum-ticket、资源与产品可达性，因此 private assets 配置较少。

### Distractor Analysis

B 大型投资者通常议价更强；C 小账户通常更容易快调，小规模不是必然慢。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** target weight 乘 AUM 才是实际投资；小账户受最低额与可达性，大账户受容量与治理资源。

可行机会集先限制 MVO，再与 liquidity budget 和 IPS 授权结合；资产规模不改变目标本身，却改变实施方式。

**Boundary：** 能满足 minimum ticket 不等于风险、费用或流动性合适；所有方案必须服从题干法律与政策限制。
