<template #prompt>

Which of the following statements **best** describes a goal-based asset allocation approach?

</template>

<template #a>

The approach derives asset allocation strategies specific to sub-portfolios

</template>

<template #b>

The approach focuses solely on the asset side of the investor's balance sheet

</template>

<template #c>

The approach applies mean-variance optimization to the surplus of investors' assets

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. The approach derives asset allocation strategies specific to sub-portfolios

### Why?

Goals-based 对每个目标建立 specific sub-portfolio，按期限与成功概率配置。

### Distractor Analysis

B 是 asset-only；C 是 liability-relative surplus optimization。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** Asset-only 看资产风险效率，liability-relative 看相对支付义务，goals-based 看目标 shortfall。

同一组 CME 可进入不同配置目标函数；Principles 中的方法选择必须继承这里的风险定义，而不能倒过来让工具决定目标。

**Boundary：** 机构也可以采用目标分层；低资产波动并不保证低 funding risk。
