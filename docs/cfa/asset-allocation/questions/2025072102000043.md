# Case 2025072102000043

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Sabonete's DB plan is closed to new employees, but existing workers still accrue benefits. It has $5bn assets and $5.55bn liabilities (90% funded). Its objectives are full funding and hedging in five years, stable annual contributions and low administrative/investment costs.

The sponsor depends heavily on emerging-market revenue and African real estate. SPP historically overweights EM because of familiarity.

Option 1 has expected return 5.5%, volatility 13%, Sharpe 0.19; Option 2 has 5.1%, 12%, 0.175. Option 1 offers a 95% chance of full funding in five years but variable interim contributions. Option 2 offers more stable contributions with a lower funding-goal probability, preferred by the CIO.

**Selected Exhibit 3 — Option 2 corridor reasoning**

Global bonds have 35% target, 10–35% range, low transaction costs, volatility 3.5% and low correlation with global equity (0.10). Radell says low volatility, low costs and low correlation all justify a wide range. He also says PE has no low-cost passive tracking vehicle and a PE index does not adequately represent actual risk.

A committee member favors Option 1 because successful pensions allegedly use the endowment model and more alternatives. The source's age/rate funded-status ranking item is excluded; Exhibit 3 also has a hedge-fund target inconsistent with Exhibit 2, which is not used in these retained questions.

Which of Radell’s justifications for the proposed rebalancing ranges for Option 2 is **most accurate**? The one based on:

</template>

<template #a>

</template>

<template #b>

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. volatility.

### Why?

低 volatility 支持较宽 band；这是 Radell 三条理由中相符的一条。

### Distractor Analysis

B 低 correlation 通常支持较窄；C 低 transaction costs 也较窄，两个都不能 justify wider。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 再平衡控制 drift；宽度平衡偏离风险与交易/税成本，高相关通常支持更宽范围。

Rebalancing 把 SAA 风险目标变成操作政策，taxes 与 liquidity 改变其成本；它与基于短期观点的 TAA 有不同目的。

**Boundary：** 说明 volatility 与 corridor 的口径；非流动投资无法套用可随时卖回目标的规则。

<template #prompt>

Radell’s statement about the portfolio allocation of private equity in Exhibit 3 is **most likely**:

</template>

<template #a>

**correct**.

</template>

<template #b>

**incorrect** in regard to a low-cost passive investment vehicle.

</template>

<template #c>

**incorrect** in regard to modeling risk and return with a private equity index.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. correct.

### Why?

PE 没有简单低成本被动复制 aggregate class 的工具，index risk/return 与 actual vehicle 也有差异，statement 在此模型语境下合适。

### Distractor Analysis

B/C 把 aggregate index 可获得误当实际可低成本精确复制。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 可锁定资本取决于提款、calls、inflows 和压力缓冲；private index 不等于 actual vehicle。

Appraisal smoothing 属于 CME 输入问题，private commitments 属于执行问题；Monte Carlo 应把 calls 和 inflow shortfalls 放入路径。

**Boundary：** 长期 horizon 不保证 liquidity；稳定 inflows 和低支出须经压力检查，premium 不是实现收益保证。
