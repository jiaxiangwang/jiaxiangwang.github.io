Fap is a small country whose currency is the Fip. Three years ago, the exchange rate was considered to be reflecting purchasing power parity (PPP). Since then, the country’s inflation has exceeded inflation in the other countries by about 5% per annum. The Fip exchange rate, however, remained broadly unchanged.
What would you have expected the Fip exchange rate to show if PPP prevailed? Are Fips over or undervalued, according to PPP?

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

PPP 预期 Fip 贬值；实际不变意味着 Fip 相对 PPP 高估。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Relative inflation 每年约高 5%，本币/外币报价应上升。
- 3 年近似累计约 15%；复利报价变化约 15.76%。
- 实际汇率不调整令本国相对价格更高，overvalued。

### Minimum Passing Answer

The Fip should depreciate by roughly 15% over three years under the approximate relative-PPP rule. With the exchange rate unchanged, it is overvalued relative to PPP.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** PPP 以相对通胀提供长期锚，资本流动可主导短期；先检查报价方向。

国际政策制度接到货币预期，再进入外币资产的本币回报；资本突然撤出也会改变 emerging debt 和 liquidity 风险。

**Boundary：** 利差可能补偿贬值或风险；PPP 偏离可以持续，短期 capital flows 并非长期购买力证明。
