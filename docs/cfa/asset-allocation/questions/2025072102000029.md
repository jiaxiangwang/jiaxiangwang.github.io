Which of the following statements about asset allocation heuristics is **most accurate**?

</template>

<template #a>

The 1/N rule exploits differences in marginal contribution to total risk

</template>

<template #b>

The endowment model characteristically seeks to earn illiquidity premiums

</template>

<template #c>

The "120 minus your age" rule is based on a hidden constant risk tolerance coefficient

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. The endowment model characteristically seeks to earn illiquidity premiums

### Why?

Endowment model 用 alternatives 与 active management 等寻求 illiquidity premiums。

### Distractor Analysis

A 1/N 不使用 MCTR；C age rule 不包含完整风险厌恶优化。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 经验法提供透明基准，但不能代替投资者目标与经济风险分析。

Heuristics 可检查 MVO 是否过于复杂或敏感；liquidity、asset size 和 liability needs 决定能否采用 endowment-style allocation。

**Boundary：** 等本金不等风险；年龄不是完整 human-capital 代理；illiquidity premium 不保证未来实现。
