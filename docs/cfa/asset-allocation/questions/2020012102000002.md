# Case · Fixed-income Risk Premiums

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

An investor will buy equal amounts of three non-callable bonds only if the portfolio risk premium over the 1-year government bond is at least 150 bp.

| Building block | Value |
| --- | --- |
| 1-year nominal government rate, including 0.6% expected inflation | 1.00% |
| 10-year term premium over the 1-year bond | 1.00% |
| 10-year BBB credit premium over the same-tenor government bond | 0.75% |
| 10-year corporate liquidity premium | 0.55% |

The candidates are the 1-year government, 10-year government and 10-year BBB corporate bonds. Use only the risk-premium approach and the data above.

## Question 1 · Constructed Response

<template #prompt>

**Calculate** the expected return that an equal-weighted investment in the three securities could provide.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

Equal-weighted expected return = 2.10%。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Nominal 1-year government return=1.00%，已包含通胀。
- 10-year government=1+1=2.00%；corporate=1+1+0.75+0.55=3.30%。
- Equal-weighted return=(1+2+3.3)/3=2.10%。

### Minimum Passing Answer

The expected return is 2.10%: (1.00% + 2.00% + 3.30%)/3. Inflation is already in the 1-year nominal rate.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 要求收益由多个 premium 构成；实现回报取决于价格与再投资，YTM 不是任意 horizon 的保证。

利率预测来自 Part 1，进入债券回报后还要看期限与信用；liability-relative allocation 则要比较资产与负债的利率暴露。

**Boundary：** duration-horizon 抵消需要简化利率变化与再投资条件；曲线扭曲、违约与交易成本会破坏近似。

### Minimum Passing Answer

The portfolio premium is 110 bp, below the 150 bp requirement; do not invest.
