Which of the following statements about the relationship of inflation and the business cycle is **most accurate**?

</template>

<template #a>

Inflation is procyclical to the business cycle

</template>

<template #b>

Inflation is independent of the business cycle

</template>

<template #c>

Inflation is countercyclical to the business cycle

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. Inflation is procyclical to the business cycle

### Why?

典型 demand-driven business cycle 中，需求与产能利用上升推动通胀，因此 procyclical。

### Distractor Analysis

B 忽略 demand pressure；C 是相反的通常关系。供给冲击可以造成 stagflation，但不改变题目所问典型周期。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 通胀影响购买力、现金流与折现率；unexpected inflation 与已定价预期不同。

通胀不仅进入 CME 的名义回报，还决定 goals-based 的未来支出与养老金负债是否需要 inflation hedging。

**Boundary：** 信用利差和租金恶化可抵消利率下降的收益；通缩不等于所有债券和股票都受益。
