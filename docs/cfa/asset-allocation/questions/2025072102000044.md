## Vignette & Exhibits

XTR targets 5.7%; the risk-free return is 1.8%.

**Exhibit 1 — Risky portfolios and matching cash weights**

| Portfolio | Expected return | Volatility | Cash weight for target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 9.5% | 18.69% | 49.35% |
| B | 8.2% | 14.95% | 39.06% |
| C | 7.8% | 14.18% | 35.00% |

Swan says MVO is factor-diversified. Gruber says expected-return error matters more than volatility/correlation error. Morrison says reverse optimization infers risk parameters from returns/correlation.

**Exhibit 2 — Williams portfolio**

| Asset | Return | Weight | Beta relative to portfolio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 10.2% | 63.34% | 1.091 |
| B | 8.4% | 10.00% | 0.857 |
| C | 8.9% | 20.00% | 0.922 |
| D | 6.3% | 6.66% | 0.584 |

Portfolio return 9.50%, volatility 21.63%, risk-free rate 1.80%.

**Exhibit 3 — Harmon goal:** $679,000 today to $775,000 in ten years with 90% success and no excess risk.

| Investment | Mean | Volatility | Annual return with 90% success |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Equity | 5.75% | 10.00% | 1.70% |
| Fixed income | 1.95% | 2.25% | 1.04% |

Gruber recommends all fixed income; Morrison says some equity is required with no further optimization; Swan says equity **and** optimization are required. The source's ambiguous illiquid-asset statements item is excluded.

### Correct Answer

B. Portfolio B.

### Why?

同 target5.7%下：σ_mix=(1−cash)σ，A≈9.467%、B≈9.111%、C≈9.217%；B 最低。

### Distractor Analysis

A 原 return 最高不代表 target mix 最优；C 原σ最低，但需要更多 risky capital。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** MVO 建有效边界，risk aversion 选 utility；加入无风险资产后比较同目标下的混合风险。

输入来自 CME，但 expected return 的小误差会被权重优化放大；下一步的 robust methods 与 simulation 分别处理输入和路径问题。

**Boundary：** 百分数与小数的 utility 系数不同；经典 tangency 结论依赖借贷与约束假设。

In the discussion of the criticisms of MVO portfolios, the **most accurate** statement is made by:

</template>

<template #a>

Swan.

</template>

<template #b>

Gruber.

</template>

<template #c>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. Gruber.

### Why?

Expected-return 输入误差通常比σ/ρ误差更显著影响 MVO weights，Gruber 合适。

### Distractor Analysis

A MVO 不保证 factor-diversified；C reverse optimization 推 implied returns，不是从 returns 反推 risk。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** reverse optimization 用 market weights 推均衡预期；Black–Litterman 再融 views；resampling 平滑权重而非消除错误。

对数据偏差与 VCV 噪声的理解决定如何选稳健方法；实际配置还要交给 liquidity 与 Monte Carlo 做可实施/路径检查。

**Boundary：** 均衡市场组合是基准不是投资者最终答案；所有方法仍依赖输入和实施假设。

<p class="source-note">Source: Local Other / Core-Asset Allocation-AA_OTH.md / No.2025072102000044-4 — adapted。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000044-4" answer="C">

<template #prompt>

Based on Exhibit 2, the marginal contribution to total risk (MCTR) of the portfolio from asset class A is **closest to**:

</template>

<template #a>

13.7%.

</template>

<template #b>

14.9%.

</template>

<template #c>

23.6%.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. 23.6%.

### Why?

MCTR_A=β_A,p σ_p=1.091×21.63%=23.598%。

### Distractor Analysis

A≈63.34%×21.63%，只是 weight×σ；B≈weight×MCTR，是 ACTR。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** MCTR 是边际风险，ACTR 是权重乘边际风险；risk parity 平分贡献而非本金。

Factor/VCV 决定 MCTR；MVO 的最优条件解释为何 marginal risk 的回报应一致，但现实约束与 liabilities 会改变预算。

**Boundary：** 相等比例条件限于适用的 interior optimum；risk parity 也不能仅凭低收益率推资本权重。

## Question 6 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local Other / Core-Asset Allocation-AA_OTH.md / No.2025072102000044-6 — adapted。</p>

<p class="source-note">原出版年份未确认；参考答案与得分点为推导，仓库无答案键。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000044-6" answer="A">

<template #prompt>

In the discussion of Harmon’s portfolio asset allocation (Exhibit 3), the **most accurate** comment is made by:

</template>

<template #a>

Swan.

</template>

<template #b>

Gruber.

</template>

<template #c>

Morrison.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. Swan.

### Why?

Required annual return=(775/679)^(1/10)−1≈1.331%；fixed-income90% minimum1.04不足，equity1.70可达。需优化混合/风险以满足90%目标，不能直接用 means 或无相关性地线性内插 percentiles。

### Distractor Analysis

B 全 FI 达不到 goal；C 说不需优化忽略最低 risk 及 mix covariance。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 按期限和成功概率选 maximum minimum return，再以其折现目标金额，汇总资金与配置。

目标框架来自 risk definition；inflation 形成未来支出，simulation 检查多期及联合 shortfall，taxes 决定净回报口径。

**Boundary：** 期初/期末、nominal/real 与每目标/联合成功概率不能混淆；正态近似不能覆盖提款路径风险。

- [用 After-tax Exposure 进行配置与 Asset Location](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/05-constraints/step-04)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 若用较高 success rate，r_min 和 PV 会怎样变化？
- 立即开始的支出第一笔应折现吗？

### Exam Takeaway

选正确期限与概率的保守回报，再谈目标资金与权重。
