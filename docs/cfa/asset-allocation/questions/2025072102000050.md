# Case 2025072102000050

## Vignette & Exhibits

The Martins, age 44, hold $600,000, with 20% cash for education beginning next year. A new four-year full scholarship removes that need. Retirement at 62 remains a high priority with baseline needs funded by 60/40 equity/bonds (±8%). They may divert 15% of future savings to a **low-priority long-term estate gift** seeking the highest return.

Interest is taxed at 35%; dividends/gains at 20%. Neal says (1) tax lowers after-tax volatility, and (2) more tax-favored assets belong in tax-deferred accounts. After three years of 20% equity returns, weights drift to 71/29. They want equivalent strategic economic risk in taxable and tax-deferred accounts; Neal expects the strong returns to continue and resists selling.

KCPF is a DB pension in a cyclical company, now overfunded after eight strong years. Its 65/35 SAA is reviewed as the economy weakens, with retaining full funding the priority. A new rule would move equity ±5% when the 50-day average crosses the 200-day average.
