<template #prompt>

Leah Sunderland, a 38-year-old software engineer, has GBP 1,000,000 in liquid financial assets, GBP 400,000 in residential real estate, and a net present value of GBP 600,000 in human capital. Which of the following **best** describes how Sunderland's residential real estate and human capital should be treated when determining her optimal asset allocation?

</template>

<template #a>

The optimizer can freely allocate any amount to her residential real estate and human capital

</template>

<template #b>

Her residential real estate and human capital should be excluded from the optimization opportunity set

</template>

<template #c>

The optimization must allocate a minimum of 20% to her residential real estate and 30% to her human capital

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. The optimization must allocate a minimum of 20% to her residential real estate and 30% to her human capital

### Why?

先用全部经济资产作分母：GBP 1,000,000 + 400,000 + 600,000 = 2,000,000。既有住宅占 20%，human capital 占 30%。题干没有出售住宅或交易未来劳动收入的安排，优化器应将这些不可自由调配的既有 exposure 作为最低配置约束，而不是重新任意分配。

### Distractor Analysis

A 把不能自由交易的既有资产当作可调配金融资产。B 可以描述证券交易清单，但若在经济资产配置模型中完全忽略它们，会漏掉既有风险。C 的 20% / 30% 顺序与数字准确对应，并未对调。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** Economic balance sheet 先恢复全部财富与义务，再确定哪些部分可以改变。Financial opportunity set 与 total economic exposure 是两个层次。

**Connection：** 工资与雇主持股共同受冲击时，新增金融投资应考虑已有 human-capital risk，而不是只优化证券账户。

**Boundary：** Minimum constraint 来自题设的不可自由交易 exposure；若允许出售住宅、调整工作收入或有义务项目，分母和限制需重建。它不是 universal recommended property allocation。
