Jo Akumba’s portfolio is invested in a range of developed markets fixed income securities. She asks her adviser about the possibility of diversifying her investments to include emerging and frontier markets government and corporate fixed income securities. Her adviser makes the following comment regarding risk:
“All emerging and frontier market fixed income securities pose economic, political and legal risk. Economic risks arise from the fact that emerging market countries have poor fiscal discipline, rely on foreign borrowing, have less diverse tax base and significant dependence on specific industries. They are susceptible to capital flight. Their ability to pay is limited. In addition, weak property rights, weak enforcement of contract laws and political instability pose hazard for emerging markets debt investors.”
**Discuss** the statement made.

### Conclusion

风险描述的方向合理，但“All”不能替代逐国/逐发行人分析。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Ability to pay：财政纪律、税基/行业集中、外币借款和资本外逃。
- Willingness / enforceability：政治不稳、财产权与合同执行。
- 不同国家、政府与公司、货币/期限风险不同，不能把所有发行人当相同风险。

### Minimum Passing Answer

These are relevant risk channels: concentrated fiscal resources and foreign borrowing weaken payment capacity, while political and legal risks weaken willingness/enforcement. Assess each issuer and currency; the risks are not uniformly present to the same degree.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 要求收益由多个 premium 构成；实现回报取决于价格与再投资，YTM 不是任意 horizon 的保证。

利率预测来自 Part 1，进入债券回报后还要看期限与信用；liability-relative allocation 则要比较资产与负债的利率暴露。

**Boundary：** duration-horizon 抵消需要简化利率变化与再投资条件；曲线扭曲、违约与交易成本会破坏近似。
