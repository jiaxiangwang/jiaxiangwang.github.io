# Case · OHF · Scale & Policy

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

OHF converts oil/gas revenue into future-generations' wealth.

- **Phase 1:** $1bn initial capital, 10% resource-tax revenue inflow, no distributions for twenty years; legally restricted to cash and IG bonds.
- **Phase 2:** transfers stop and income funds development/social uses. The mandate expands into companies, property and infrastructure; external managers are retained if they beat sector peers during the prior two years.
- **Phase 3:** resource transfers resume at 35%. Half the portfolio is passive public equity; alternatives use outside managers, limited to about $75m per manager. Assets rise to $28bn and investments must be abroad. Policy changes require legislative supermajorities.
- **Phase 4:** management believes lower oil prices and production will persist through multiple cycles. It obtains approval for reduced withdrawals, higher equity and ±5% tactical flexibility while retaining long-term fund objectives.

The source's incremental-TAA-return item is excluded: key weights and a referenced exhibit are missing.

<template #prompt>

During Phase 2, OHF’s external manager selection and retention policy was **most likely** susceptible to which of the following biases?

</template>

<template #a>

Endowment bias

</template>

<template #b>

Loss aversion bias

</template>

<template #c>

Representative bias

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. Representative bias

### Why?

仅依据最近两年 sector outperformer 选择/保留经理，把短记录当持久 skill，representativeness。

### Distractor Analysis

A 拥有某物导致偏爱；B 害怕亏损；直接证据是 recent winner extrapolation。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 用具体行为识别 bias，并说明其对预测、配置或执行的影响。

Bias 可以污染 CME 或阻止 rebalancing；goals-based 与书面治理规则帮助执行，但仍要检查经济资产负债表的总体暴露。

**Boundary：** 目标分层可以合理；认知纠偏与情绪适配不同，不能用同一教育话术处理所有客户。

## Question 4 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local Other / Core-Asset Allocation-AA_OTH.md / No.2025072102000049-4 — adapted。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000049-4" answer="B">

<template #prompt>

The changes that were allowed in OHF’s strategic asset allocation in Phase 4 are **best** classified as relating to changes in:

</template>

<template #a>

goals.

</template>

<template #b>

beliefs.

</template>

<template #c>

constraints.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. beliefs.

### Why?

Management 认为 oil price/production 低迷跨多个 cycles 持续，长期 return/funding belief 变化是主要 trigger。

### Distractor Analysis

A 长期目标仍保留；C 实际获准权重变化是响应，不是最初 economic driver。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** SAA review 区分 goals、constraints、long-term beliefs；事件分类依题干驱动，不依固定标签。

Horizon 改变 human capital、目标 PV 与 sequence risk；需要重新检查 goals-based 和 liability funding，而非只移动比例。

**Boundary：** 一个事件能影响多项，问 primary reason 时抓最直接证据；远期 contingency goal 与即时 emergency cash 不同。
