## Vignette & Exhibits

Beade reviews an MVO allocation notably different from market weights.

**Selected Exhibit 1 — Allocation vs market weights (%)**

| Class | Portfolio | Global market |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US bonds | 30 | 17 |
| Emerging equity | 25 | 5 |
| Non-US developed equity | 20 | 29 |
| US small/mid equity | 25 | 4 |

Other market classes are omitted from this selected comparison; missing allocation cells are not reconstructed.

A pension has assets $205m, liability PV $241m. Both are expected to fall $25m. For surplus utility use the formula confirmed in the same Case's local 原版书 export: $U=E(R_S)-0.005\lambda\sigma_S^2$ with percent inputs and $\lambda=1.50$.

**Exhibit 2 — Surplus portfolios**

| Portfolio | Return | Volatility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 13% | 24% |
| 2 | 12% | 18% |
| 3 | 11% | 19% |

Kealoha has much more wealth than needed for a fixed ten-year payment and wants reliable funding plus competitive returns on remaining assets.

**Exhibit 3 — Five-year goals portfolios**

| Input | A | B | C | D | E |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mean | 4.60% | 5.80% | 7.00% | 8.20% | 9.40% |
| Volatility | 3.46% | 5.51% | 8.08% | 10.80% | 13.59% |
| 95% minimum return | 2.05% | 1.75% | 1.06% | 0.25% | −0.60% |
| 85% minimum return | 3.00% | 3.25% | 3.26% | 3.19% | 3.10% |

Rodríguez wants maximum minimum return at 95% for Goal 1 and 85% for Goal 2.

Müller's comments: (1) 120-age adds an age-based allocation to 60/40; (2) Yale emphasizes traditional assets and active management; (3) 1/N depends on asset investment characteristics. The corridor-conflict and incomplete risk-parity-weight items are excluded individually.

<template #prompt>

The asset allocation in Exhibit 1 **most likely** resulted from a mean–variance optimization using:

</template>

<template #a>

historical data.

</template>

<template #b>

reverse optimization.

</template>

<template #c>

Black–Litterman inputs.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. historical data.

### Why?

大幅集中于 EM 与 US small/mid 并远离 market weights，符合历史μ噪声驱动 MVO 的典型极端输出。

### Distractor Analysis

B equilibrium reverse inputs 通常更接近 market；C 结合 market prior 的 views 通常较温和。仅为题干的 most-likely 识别，不能由权重唯一证明模型。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** reverse optimization 用 market weights 推均衡预期；Black–Litterman 再融 views；resampling 平滑权重而非消除错误。

对数据偏差与 VCV 噪声的理解决定如何选稳健方法；实际配置还要交给 liquidity 与 Monte Carlo 做可实施/路径检查。

**Boundary：** 均衡市场组合是基准不是投资者最终答案；所有方法仍依赖输入和实施假设。

### Exam Takeaway

稳健方法约束输入噪声，不能把错误假设变真。

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000046-3" answer="A">

<template #prompt>

Based on Beade’s interest rate expectations, the pension plan’s funding ratio will:

</template>

<template #a>

decrease.

</template>

<template #b>

remain unchanged.

</template>

<template #c>

increase.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. decrease.

### Why?

205/241=85.06%；(205−25)/(241−25)=83.33%，funding ratio 下降。

### Distractor Analysis

B 只看 surplus−36不变；C 误认为 liabilities 减少必然改善 funding。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 资产相对支付义务的风险决定方法；surplus 与 funding ratio 反映不同信息。

经济资产负债表同时识别 sponsor 经营风险；CME 的 rates/inflation 进入负债价值，governance 决定目标优先级和贡献政策。

**Boundary：** 负债估值与 cash-flow 风险不能用 asset Sharpe 替代；等额资产负债变化不保持 ratio，closed 不等于 frozen。

</template>

<template #a>

Portfolio 1

</template>

<template #b>

</template>

<template #c>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. Portfolio 2

### Why?

百分数 utility 为13−.005×1.5×24²=8.68；12−.005×1.5×18²=9.57；11−.005×1.5×19²=8.2925；P2最高。

### Distractor Analysis

Amean 最高但 risk penalty 大；C 较低 mean 也未补足 risk efficiency。公式 OCR 用同源 ORIG 清晰版本校验。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** MVO 建有效边界，risk aversion 选 utility；加入无风险资产后比较同目标下的混合风险。

输入来自 CME，但 expected return 的小误差会被权重优化放大；下一步的 robust methods 与 simulation 分别处理输入和路径问题。

**Boundary：** 百分数与小数的 utility 系数不同；经典 tangency 结论依赖借贷与约束假设。

### Exam Takeaway

先保证效率，再用风险偏好与约束决定配置。

<template #prompt>

Based on Exhibit 3, which subportfolios **best** meet the two goals expressed by client Rodríguez?

</template>

<template #a>

Subportfolio A for Goal 1 and Subportfolio C for Goal 2

</template>

<template #b>

Subportfolio B for Goal 1 and Subportfolio C for Goal 2

</template>

<template #c>

Subportfolio E for Goal 1 and Subportfolio A for Goal 2

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. Subportfolio A for Goal 1 and Subportfolio C for Goal 2

### Why?

95%row 最大 r_min A2.05%；85%row 最大 C3.26%。按每 goal 对应 row 选，不按 mean 最高。

### Distractor Analysis

B 的95%floor1.75较低；C 把高 risk E 给95%保守目标，floor−0.60最低。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 按期限和成功概率选 maximum minimum return，再以其折现目标金额，汇总资金与配置。

目标框架来自 risk definition；inflation 形成未来支出，simulation 检查多期及联合 shortfall，taxes 决定净回报口径。

**Boundary：** 期初/期末、nominal/real 与每目标/联合成功概率不能混淆；正态近似不能覆盖提款路径风险。

<template #a>

Comment 1

</template>

<template #b>

A. Comment 1

### Why?

**Boundary：** 等本金不等风险；年龄不是完整 human-capital 代理；illiquidity premium 不保证未来实现。
