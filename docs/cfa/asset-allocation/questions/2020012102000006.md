**Identify** risks faced by investors in emerging market equities over and above those that are faced by fixed income investors in such markets.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

额外关注少数股东治理保护、盈利竞争力与会计披露质量。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Equity residual claims 更受 controlling-shareholder / corporate-governance 风险影响。
- 弱竞争力与会计不透明直接影响 earnings、valuation 与权益持有人。
- 政治/法律风险同时影响债券；需要说明股权更敏感的具体渠道。

### Minimum Passing Answer

Equity investors have greater exposure to weak corporate governance/minority-shareholder protection, competitive earnings risk and unreliable accounting disclosures.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** G–K 拆分收入、增长、股本与估值；Singer–Terhaar 根据市场整合程度区分系统风险与总风险补偿。

长期盈利输入接自 trend growth；全球整合和治理风险又影响 robust MVO 的预期与相关性假设。

**Boundary：** 整合过渡的估值增益不等于之后的均衡回报；required premium 本身不能证明资产便宜。
