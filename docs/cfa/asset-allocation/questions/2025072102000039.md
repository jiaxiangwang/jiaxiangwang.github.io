# Case 2025072102000039

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Vitting University is considering a $210.3m capital project. A donor will provide $50m if it proceeds.

**Exhibit 1 — Economic assets and obligations ($m)**

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Immediately available, unrestricted | 50.2 |
| Immediately available, scholarships only | 53.2 |
| Current obligations | 89.5 |
| PV of future obligations | 502.4 |
| PV of future unrestricted pledges | 505.4 |
| PV of scholarship-only pledges | 87.8 |

Future unrestricted funds/obligations may include scholarship and non-scholarship items. Restricted funds retain their designated purpose.

Black recommends fixed-income cash distributions to offset university expenditure not covered by tuition, and ±5% weight deviations to exploit short-term opportunities.

A committee of arts/sciences faculty and final-year students will draft the IPS. The board approves it, advisers/managers implement it, and progress/governance reports are supplied only on request. Faculty serve non-renewable two-year terms; investment expertise is not specified.

Which asset allocation approach **best** describes the asset allocation choice suggested by Black to the board?

</template>

<template #a>

</template>

<template #b>

Mean-variance

</template>

<template #c>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. Liability-relative

### Why?

建议 fixed-income cash distributions 抵销大学未来必要支出，目标是 liability-relative matching。

### Distractor Analysis

B mean-variance 不直接突出支付匹配；C 不是题干具体 liability-risk 框架。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** Asset-only 看资产风险效率，liability-relative 看相对支付义务，goals-based 看目标 shortfall。

同一组 CME 可进入不同配置目标函数；Principles 中的方法选择必须继承这里的风险定义，而不能倒过来让工具决定目标。

**Boundary：** 机构也可以采用目标分层；低资产波动并不保证低 funding risk。

Black’s suggestion to the board in regard to the asset weightings in the endowment portfolio is **best** described as allowing for an asset allocation that is:

</template>

<template #a>

tactical.

<template #b>

Static.

</template>

<template #c>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. tactical.

### Why?

题干明确短期机会以额外 return 为目的的 ±5% 偏离，是 tactical。

### Distractor Analysis

B static 无主动短期 view；C dynamic 不专指题干的 short-term opportunity tilt。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** TAA 在 IPS 内利用短期观点；策略比较以 SAA 为基准并扣额外实施成本。

CME 提供战术输入，governance 限定授权，liquidity/tax 决定实施代价；rebalancing 是无战术观点时的风险恢复机制。

**Boundary：** 更高 alpha 不允许越界；持久长期环境改变可能需要 SAA review，而不是永久 tactical tilt。

The process for creating and implementing the investment policy statement (IPS) by the University Planning and Priorities Committee (UPPC) **most likely** follows **best** governance practices in regard to:

</template>

<template #a>

</template>

<template #b>

expertise for developing the asset allocation.

</template>

<template #c>

transparency of decision rights for approving a proposed asset allocation..

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. transparency of decision rights for approving a proposed asset allocation..

### Why?

IPS 起草→board approval→managers implementation 的权限明确，是 transparency of decision rights。

### Distractor Analysis

A reports only upon request 并非充分周期监督；B 成员无明确投资 expertise，不可据此声称专业性好。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** IPS 定义目标与授权，SAA 决定长期政策风险，TAA 与 rebalancing 在不同触发下实施。

治理先于经济资产负债表与优化；后续 Real-World Constraints 中的 TAA、税务与行为政策都须落在授权范围内。

**Boundary：** 委员会透明授权是一项治理优点，不证明其成员专业能力与监督机制也充分。
