# Case · Monteo · Utility & Risk Budgets

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Monteo estimates Perkins' risk aversion $\lambda=8$ and uses percent-input utility $U=\mu-0.005\lambda\sigma^2$.

**Exhibit 1**

| Allocation | Expected return | Volatility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 10% | 12% |
| B | 8% | 8% |
| C | 6% | 2% |

Velky wants direct property, infrastructure and PE. He says: (1) optimal risk budgets minimize total risk; (2) risk budgeting decomposes total risk; (3) optimal allocations have different excess-return/MCTR ratios for every asset.

Chaterji criticizes MVO as (1) highly input-sensitive and (2) highly dispersed across all classes. He describes investment factors as (1) generally low-correlated with the market and each other, and (2) different from fundamental/structural factors used in multifactor models.

<template #prompt>

Based on Exhibit 1 and the risk aversion coefficient, the preferred asset allocation for Perkins is:

</template>

<template #a>

Asset Allocation A.

</template>

<template #b>

Asset Allocation B.

</template>

<template #c>

Asset Allocation C.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. Asset Allocation C.

### Why?

λ=8：U_A=10−.005×8×12²=4.24；U_B=8−.005×8×8²=5.44；U_C=6−.005×8×2²=5.84%。C 最高。

### Distractor Analysis

A 最高 mean 非最高 utility；B 风险虽低于 A 但效用仍低于 C。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** MVO 建有效边界，risk aversion 选 utility；加入无风险资产后比较同目标下的混合风险。

输入来自 CME，但 expected return 的小误差会被权重优化放大；下一步的 robust methods 与 simulation 分别处理输入和路径问题。

**Boundary：** 百分数与小数的 utility 系数不同；经典 tangency 结论依赖借贷与约束假设。

- [用 MCTR 区分 Capital Weight 与 Risk Weight](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/04-principles/step-04)

- 目标回报低于最高 Sharpe 组合时，为什么不直接选最低波动风险组合？
- 若禁止 leverage，target return 高于 tangency return 怎么办？

### Exam Takeaway

先保证效率，再用风险偏好与约束决定配置。

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000047-2" answer="C">

<template #prompt>

In their discussion of the asset classes that Velky is interested in adding to the VP portfolio, Monteo should tell Velky that:

</template>

<template #a>

these asset classes can be readily diversified to eliminate idiosyncratic risk.

</template>

<template #b>

indexes are available for these asset classes that do an outstanding job of representing the performance characteristics of the asset classes.

</template>

<template #c>

the risk and return characteristics associated with actual investment vehicles for these asset classes are typically significantly different from the characteristics of the asset classes themselves.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. the risk and return characteristics associated with actual investment vehicles for these asset classes are typically significantly different from the characteristics of the asset classes themselves.

### Why?

Actual private vehicles 含经理、集中、费用、leverage 和结构风险，可能显著偏离 aggregate class statistics。

### Distractor Analysis

A 实际 vehicle 不易彻底分散；B 历史 index 不保证代表 actual risk/return，常有 smoothing。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 可锁定资本取决于提款、calls、inflows 和压力缓冲；private index 不等于 actual vehicle。

Appraisal smoothing 属于 CME 输入问题，private commitments 属于执行问题；Monte Carlo 应把 calls 和 inflow shortfalls 放入路径。

**Boundary：** 长期 horizon 不保证 liquidity；稳定 inflows 和低支出须经压力检查，premium 不是实现收益保证。

- 捐赠和 tuition 同时下降，为何会双重恶化 liquidity？

### Exam Takeaway

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. Statement 2

### Why?

Risk budgeting 分解 total portfolio risk，是 Statement2。

### Distractor Analysis

A 忽略 return 补偿与投资目标；C interior optimum 应相等而非 different，binding constraints 需另看。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** MCTR 是边际风险，ACTR 是权重乘边际风险；risk parity 平分贡献而非本金。

Factor/VCV 决定 MCTR；MVO 的最优条件解释为何 marginal risk 的回报应一致，但现实约束与 liabilities 会改变预算。

**Boundary：** 相等比例条件限于适用的 interior optimum；risk parity 也不能仅凭低收益率推资本权重。

</template>

<template #b>

Only Criticism 2

<template #c>

### Correct Answer

A. Only Criticism 1

### Why?

MVO 敏感于 input changes 是真问题；常见是集中/极端权重，而非自然 highly dispersed。

### Distractor Analysis

B/C 认可了错误 Criticism2。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** reverse optimization 用 market weights 推均衡预期；Black–Litterman 再融 views；resampling 平滑权重而非消除错误。

对数据偏差与 VCV 噪声的理解决定如何选稳健方法；实际配置还要交给 liquidity 与 Monte Carlo 做可实施/路径检查。

**Boundary：** 均衡市场组合是基准不是投资者最终答案；所有方法仍依赖输入和实施假设。

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000047-5" answer="C">

<template #prompt>

Which of the characteristics put forth by Chaterji to describe the factor-based approach is/are **correct**?

</template>

<template #a>

Only Characteristic 1

</template>

<template #b>

Only Characteristic 2

</template>

<template #c>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. Both Characteristic 1 and Characteristic 2

### Why?

题干讨论 investment factors：设计上寻求与 market 及彼此较低相关，且常不同于 macro/structural risk factors；两项在这种教学定义下成立。

### Distractor Analysis

A/B 各漏一项；不是所有因子/所有时期的低相关保证。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 因子模型揭示跨资产共同风险；经济风险因素与可实施 investment-factor portfolios 要区分。

VCV 的 factor model 是风险估计工具，risk budgeting 用共同风险分配贡献；两者让资产标签下的暴露进入量化配置。

**Boundary：** 因子并非天然可交易，也不保证稳定低相关；实现工具会有剩余风险与成本。

- Factor exposure 可否直接当作一只可买证券？
