## Vignette & Exhibits

The Laws, ages 42/44, both work for WS. Equities total $800,000, 80% in WS shares; bonds $450,000; property $400,000; mortgage $225,000; human-capital PV $1,025,000; consumption PV $750,000. Essential education has PV $275,000 and a desired university endowment in twenty years has PV $500,000 with a high success requirement.

The prior adviser defines equity as US stocks, debt as global IG corporate bonds **and property**, and derivatives as mostly large foreign equities. Equity/derivative returns are highly correlated; debt is less correlated. Raye wants to control inflation, liquidity and volatility factors.

**Exhibit 1 — Candidate goals portfolios**

| Portfolio | Cash | Bonds | Global equity | Hedge funds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 35% | 55% | 10% | 0% |
| 2 | 10% | 15% | 65% | 10% |
| 3 | 10% | 30% | 40% | 20% |

Raye generally considers 75% equity/25% bonds appropriate for a twenty-year **moderately important** goal. He proposes wider global-equity rebalancing ranges using a cost–benefit approach. The source's endowment-choice item is excluded because these data do not reliably determine its required high-success-rate portfolio.

Raye’s approach to rebalancing global equities is consistent with:

</template>

</template>

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. global equities’ having higher transaction costs than other asset classes.

### Why?

成本—收益再平衡中 higher transaction costs 支持 wider range。

### Distractor Analysis

A risk aversion 通常更窄；C 低 correlation 提高保持分散的价值，通常更窄。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 再平衡控制 drift；宽度平衡偏离风险与交易/税成本，高相关通常支持更宽范围。

Rebalancing 把 SAA 风险目标变成操作政策，taxes 与 liquidity 改变其成本；它与基于短期观点的 TAA 有不同目的。

**Boundary：** 说明 volatility 与 corridor 的口径；非流动投资无法套用可随时卖回目标的规则。
