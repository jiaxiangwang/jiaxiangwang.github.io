An investor with a 3-year investment horizon purchases a fixed rate bond with a 5-year Macaulay duration. If there is a significant change in interest rates, the realized return of the bond investment **most likely** will be:

</template>

<template #a>

less sensitive to the impact of reinvestment income than the impact of capital gains/losses.

</template>

<template #b>

unchanged because profit or loss from reinvestment will approximately equal the gains or losses from capital gains.

</template>

<template #c>

more sensitive to the impact of reinvestment income than the impact of capital gains/losses.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. less sensitive to the impact of reinvestment income than the impact of capital gains/losses.

### Why?

H=3 小于 Macaulay Duration=5；价格变动对较近期限实现回报的影响大于 coupon reinvestment。

### Distractor Analysis

B 需要 horizon≈duration 等简化抵消条件；C 更符合 horizon 长于 duration 的情况。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 要求收益由多个 premium 构成；实现回报取决于价格与再投资，YTM 不是任意 horizon 的保证。

利率预测来自 Part 1，进入债券回报后还要看期限与信用；liability-relative allocation 则要比较资产与负债的利率暴露。

**Boundary：** duration-horizon 抵消需要简化利率变化与再投资条件；曲线扭曲、违约与交易成本会破坏近似。
