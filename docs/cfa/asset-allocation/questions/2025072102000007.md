A common tilt when implementing the global market portfolio is to overweight:

</template>

<template #a>

growth stocks relative to value stocks.

</template>

<template #b>

large capitalization stocks relative to small capitalization stocks.

</template>

<template #c>

the investor's home-country market relative to non-home-country market.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. the investor's home-country market relative to non-home-country market.

### Why?

实际 global market portfolio 实施常见 home-country overweight：familiarity、tax、currency 或治理原因可能导致 tilt。

### Distractor Analysis

A/B 不是题问最常见的 home bias；不能把 home tilt 一律证明合理。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** reverse optimization 用 market weights 推均衡预期；Black–Litterman 再融 views；resampling 平滑权重而非消除错误。

对数据偏差与 VCV 噪声的理解决定如何选稳健方法；实际配置还要交给 liquidity 与 Monte Carlo 做可实施/路径检查。

**Boundary：** 均衡市场组合是基准不是投资者最终答案；所有方法仍依赖输入和实施假设。
