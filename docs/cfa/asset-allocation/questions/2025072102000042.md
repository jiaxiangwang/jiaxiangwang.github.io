# Case · Shipman & Sebastian · Goal Funding

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Shipman invests $900,000 and wants a $54,000 withdrawal in one year **without reducing principal**.

**Exhibit 1 — Tax-adjusted forecasts**

| Portfolio | Return | Volatility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10.50% | 20.0% |
| 2 | 9.00% | 13.0% |
| 3 | 7.75% | 10.0% |

Remington says higher transaction costs and correlation with the rest of the portfolio justify wider corridors. Montgomery says higher volatility in the rest of the portfolio also justifies wider corridors.

Winfield's allocation starts from reverse-optimized global-market weights with return adjustments for her views. Remington says resampled MVO combines MVO with Monte Carlo and produces more diversified weights. Montgomery criticizes it as producing under-diversified riskier allocations and inheriting original input errors.

Montgomery also says: (1) 60/40 optimizes growth/risk reduction; (2) Norway is an active public-market variation of endowment investing incorporating ESG; (3) 1/N equally weights classes and rebalances without using return, volatility or correlation.

Sebastian has $2.9m. Goal 1: $120,000 annual living spending for twenty years, beginning **immediately**, inflation 2.5%, success 90%. Goal 2: $1.5m **nominal** donation in ten years, success 85%.

**Exhibit 2 — Minimum expectation returns**

| Input | BY | CZ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mean | 5.70% | 7.10% |
| Volatility | 5.10% | 7.40% |
| Current weight | 40% | 60% |
| 10 years / 99% | 2.90% | 2.50% |
| 10 years / 90% | 3.40% | 2.80% |
| 10 years / 85% | 3.60% | 3.00% |
| 20 years / 95% | 5.10% | 5.40% |
| 20 years / 90% | 5.20% | 5.70% |
| 20 years / 85% | 5.60% | 5.90% |

Assume zero correlation between the horizon portfolios.

<template #prompt>

Which of the portfolios provided in Exhibit 1 has the **highest** probability of enabling Shipman to meet his goal for the vacation home?

</template>

<template #a>

Portfolio 1

</template>

<template #b>

Portfolio 2

</template>

<template #c>

Portfolio 3

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. Portfolio 2

### Why?

Required return=54,000/900,000=6%；safety-first (μ−6%)/σ为0.225、0.23077、0.175，P2最大。

### Distractor Analysis

A mean 最高但σ也高；C σ最低但相对6%目标的 risk-adjusted buffer 较小。此概率排序用题目简化同形分布。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 在相同正态/可比较分布假设下，最大化 $SFR=(\mu-R_L)/\sigma$ 可降低低于实际目标的概率。这里的 $R_L$ 来自支出或指定目标，不自动等于 $r_f$。

**Connection：** Sharpe 的门槛是无风险回报，Safety-First 的门槛是投资者的最低要求；多期 goals-based 则用匹配概率/期限的 minimum expectation return 计算 funding cost。

**Boundary：** 只给 mean/volatility 不能在任意非正态分布下严格排序概率。Cash-flow paths 和 tail risk 应用 simulation 检查。

- [选择与目标一致的 Risk Definition](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/03-overview/step-03)
- [用 Scenario / Monte Carlo 检查路径与目标风险](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/04-principles/step-03)
- [用 After-tax Exposure 进行配置与 Asset Location](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/05-constraints/step-04)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 如果 minimum target 改成 risk-free return，是否变为 Sharpe ranking？
- 如果 return distributions 有不同 skew/tails，SFR 能否证明概率排名？

### Exam Takeaway

先找实际 target threshold；buffer 除 volatility，不以最高均值或最高 Sharpe 替代目标成功率。

## Question 2 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local Other / Core-Asset Allocation-AA_OTH.md / No.2025072102000042-2 — adapted。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000042-2" answer="A">

<template #prompt>

When discussing asset allocation corridors with Shipman, which of Remington’s and Montgomery’s statements is the **least accurate**? The one regarding:

</template>

<template #a>

volatility.

</template>

<template #b>

correlation.

</template>

<template #c>

transaction costs.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. volatility.

### Why?

higher rest-of-portfolio volatility 在总体风险偏离成本权衡下通常支持更窄 band；Montgomery 说更宽不合。

### Distractor Analysis

B higher correlation、C higher transaction costs 均支持更宽。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 再平衡控制 drift；宽度平衡偏离风险与交易/税成本，高相关通常支持更宽范围。

Rebalancing 把 SAA 风险目标变成操作政策，taxes 与 liquidity 改变其成本；它与基于短期观点的 TAA 有不同目的。

**Boundary：** 说明 volatility 与 corridor 的口径；非流动投资无法套用可随时卖回目标的规则。

- 若交易成本上升，corridor 通常如何变？
- 高度相关资产一起涨跌，为何可以容忍更宽范围？

### Exam Takeaway

交易成本提高使范围更宽，风险偏离更昂贵则更窄。

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000042-3" answer="A">

<template #prompt>

The model on which Winfield’s current asset allocation is based is **best** characterized as:

</template>

<template #a>

Black-Litterman.

</template>

<template #b>

reverse optimization.

</template>

<template #c>

mean-variance optimization.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. Black-Litterman.

### Why?

先 global-market reverse equilibrium 再 views 调整，属于 Black–Litterman。

### Distractor Analysis

B 只有均衡基准；C 未体现市场先验+views 结合。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** reverse optimization 用 market weights 推均衡预期；Black–Litterman 再融 views；resampling 平滑权重而非消除错误。

对数据偏差与 VCV 噪声的理解决定如何选稳健方法；实际配置还要交给 liquidity 与 Monte Carlo 做可实施/路径检查。

**Boundary：** 均衡市场组合是基准不是投资者最终答案；所有方法仍依赖输入和实施假设。

## Question 6 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local Other / Core-Asset Allocation-AA_OTH.md / No.2025072102000042-6 — adapted。</p>

<p class="source-note">原出版年份未确认；参考答案与得分点为推导，仓库无答案键。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000042-6" answer="C">

<template #prompt>

Using Exhibit 2, which of the sub-portfolio allocations is **most likely** to meet both of Sebastian’s goals?

</template>

<template #a>

The current sub-portfolio allocation

</template>

<template #b>

A 43% allocation to sub-portfolio BY and a 57% allocation to sub-portfolio CZ

</template>

<template #c>

A 37% allocation to sub-portfolio BY and a 63% allocation to sub-portfolio CZ

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. A 37% allocation to sub-portfolio BY and a 63% allocation to sub-portfolio CZ

### Why?

Living goal 用 CZ 的20y/90% r_min=5.7%，期初通胀支出 PV≈1.820446m，占62.77%；donation 用 BY 的10y/85%3.6%，PV≈1.053158m，占36.32%。37%BY/63%CZ 覆盖两者。

### Distractor Analysis

A40/60的 CZ 不足生活资金；B43/57更不足。不能用平均5.7/7.1或把立即支出多折一年。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 按期限和成功概率选 maximum minimum return，再以其折现目标金额，汇总资金与配置。

目标框架来自 risk definition；inflation 形成未来支出，simulation 检查多期及联合 shortfall，taxes 决定净回报口径。

**Boundary：** 期初/期末、nominal/real 与每目标/联合成功概率不能混淆；正态近似不能覆盖提款路径风险。

- [选择与目标一致的 Risk Definition](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/03-overview/step-03)
- [用 Scenario / Monte Carlo 检查路径与目标风险](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/04-principles/step-03)
- [用 After-tax Exposure 进行配置与 Asset Location](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/05-constraints/step-04)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 若用较高 success rate，r_min 和 PV 会怎样变化？
- 立即开始的支出第一笔应折现吗？

### Exam Takeaway

选正确期限与概率的保守回报，再谈目标资金与权重。
