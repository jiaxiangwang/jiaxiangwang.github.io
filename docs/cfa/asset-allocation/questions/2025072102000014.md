<template #prompt>

A defined benefit pension plan whose benefit payments depend on the employees' retirement dates, longevity and cash payout rules is **most likely** considered a:

</template>

<template #a>

contingent and legal liability to the company.

</template>

<template #b>

non-contingent and legal liability to the company.

</template>

<template #c>

non-contingent and quasi-liability to the company.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. contingent and legal liability to the company.

### Why?

DB obligation 是 legal；支付取决于 retirement、longevity 与规则，所以 contingent。

### Distractor Analysis

B/C 均错误地把随机现金流当 non-contingent，C 还把 legal debt 当 quasi-liability。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 完整现值资产减负债得到经济净值；既有收入、公司股、地产暴露决定实际风险容量。

经济资产负债表用于选择 asset-only / liability-relative / goals-based；corporate sponsor 的经营风险也会影响养老金配置。

**Boundary：** 受限捐款不能挪用，future inflows 不是当前现金；非交易 human capital 应作为既有暴露而非自由调权工具。
