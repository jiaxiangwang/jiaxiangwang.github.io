Which of the following statements related to the trend growth rate is **most accurate**? In the long run, the:

</template>

<template #a>

trend growth rate provides an appropriate way to estimate the dividend yield.

</template>

<template #b>

growth rate of the total value of equity is linked to the growth of capital's share of income.

</template>

<template #c>

average level of real default-free bond yields will be pulled toward the trend rate of real growth.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. average level of real default-free bond yields will be pulled toward the trend rate of real growth.

### Why?

长期 real default-free yields 受趋势真实增长机会牵引；这是长期锚，非每期等式。

### Distractor Analysis

A 将增长与 dividend yield 混淆，股息率还受价格与 payout 影响；B 漏掉总经济增长，只看 capital share。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 真实趋势增长近似为劳动投入增长加生产率增长；长期盈利需要经济支持。

把趋势增长接到 Grinold–Kroner，可检验回报究竟来自经营增长、股息还是估值变化。

**Boundary：** 趋势锚不是逐年预测；利润份额与股份数量变动会改变每股收益。

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 利润占 GDP 比例短期上升时，能否把盈利增速机械锁在 GDP？
- 如果 P/E 不变，给定增长和股息后预期回报是多少？

### Exam Takeaway

长期预期必须能持续，不能永远依赖利润份额或估值倍数上升。
