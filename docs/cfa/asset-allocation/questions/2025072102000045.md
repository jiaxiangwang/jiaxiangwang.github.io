# Case 2025072102000045

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

VU wants the allocation with the highest probability of reaching **5% annual return**, using expected return and volatility. The risk-free rate is 1.2%.

**Exhibit 2 — Allocation candidates**

| Allocation | Mean | Volatility | CAPM beta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6.3% | 19.5% | 1.82 |
| 2 | 7.2% | 28.6% | 1.76 |
| 3 | 8.1% | 44.2% | 1.68 |

Black recommends Monte Carlo to include rebalancing costs and distributions not fully characterized by mean and volatility. This case uses the target-return data, not the duplicated source's unrelated capital-project calculations.

### Correct Answer

B. 2.

### Why?

用 Roy safety-first ratio (μ−5)/σ，分别0.06667、0.07692、0.07014，Allocation2最大。

### Distractor Analysis

A 的 Sharpe 更高，但 threshold 是5%而非 rf1.2%；Cmean 最高但 target-adjusted buffer 不最高。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 在相同正态/可比较分布假设下，最大化 $SFR=(\mu-R_L)/\sigma$ 可降低低于实际目标的概率。这里的 $R_L$ 来自支出或指定目标，不自动等于 $r_f$。

**Connection：** Sharpe 的门槛是无风险回报，Safety-First 的门槛是投资者的最低要求；多期 goals-based 则用匹配概率/期限的 minimum expectation return 计算 funding cost。

**Boundary：** 只给 mean/volatility 不能在任意非正态分布下严格排序概率。Cash-flow paths 和 tail risk 应用 simulation 检查。

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. correct.

### Why?

Monte Carlo 能显式加入交易成本和不止 mean/variance 的 joint distribution，只要模型定义这些特征。

### Distractor Analysis

B/C 都是“不能处理”的错误限制；能够处理不等于输入可靠或 cost 自动被包含。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 有现金流时回报顺序影响 wealth；simulation 检验路径风险，scenario 解释特定冲击机制。

MVO 选单期效率，goals-based 定成功概率，Monte Carlo 检查多期实现；liquidity 与 rebalancing 规则必须在路径里体现。

**Boundary：** 模拟概率取决于指定模型；次数再多也不能弥补缺失的 tail、税与现金流假设。
