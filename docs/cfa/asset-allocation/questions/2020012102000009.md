A client has asked his adviser to explain the key considerations in forecasting exchange rates. The adviser’s firm uses two broad complementary approaches when setting expectations for exchange rate movements, namely focus on trade in goods and services and, secondly, focus on capital flows. **Identify** the main considerations that the adviser should explain to the client under the two approaches.

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

同时解释 trade / PPP 的长期锚与 capital-flow 的短期力量。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Trade：relative inflation、竞争力与 current-account sustainability。
- Capital flows：相对增长/预期收益、资本开放、risk premium 与政策可信度。
- 短期资金流可造成 overshooting，不能把 PPP 当短期保证。

### Minimum Passing Answer

Trade analysis examines relative inflation, competitiveness and external-balance sustainability. Capital-flow analysis examines expected investment returns, growth, openness and risk/credibility; flows can dominate short-run exchange rates.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** PPP 以相对通胀提供长期锚，资本流动可主导短期；先检查报价方向。

国际政策制度接到货币预期，再进入外币资产的本币回报；资本突然撤出也会改变 emerging debt 和 liquidity 风险。

**Boundary：** 利差可能补偿贬值或风险；PPP 偏离可以持续，短期 capital flows 并非长期购买力证明。
