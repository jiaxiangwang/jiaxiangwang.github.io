The **primary** objective of tactical asset allocation is to:

</template>

<template #a>

establish long-term investment policy targets for asset class weights.

</template>

<template #b>

establish the most efficient asset class mixes in the presence of liabilities.

</template>

<template #c>

increase risk-adjusted return by taking advantage of short-term economic and financial market conditions.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. increase risk-adjusted return by taking advantage of short-term economic and financial market conditions.

### Why?

TAA 在短期相对市场条件下主动偏离以提升 risk-adjusted return。

### Distractor Analysis

A 是 SAA；B 是 liability-relative 配置效率问题。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** TAA 在 IPS 内利用短期观点；策略比较以 SAA 为基准并扣额外实施成本。

CME 提供战术输入，governance 限定授权，liquidity/tax 决定实施代价；rebalancing 是无战术观点时的风险恢复机制。

**Boundary：** 更高 alpha 不允许越界；持久长期环境改变可能需要 SAA review，而不是永久 tactical tilt。
