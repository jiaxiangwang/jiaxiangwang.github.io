Which of the following statements about using factors in the asset allocation process is **most accurate**?

Risk factors are directly investable

</template>

### Correct Answer

B. Multifactor risk models help control systematic risk exposures in asset allocation

### Why?

Multifactor models 揭示和管理共同 systematic exposures。

### Distractor Analysis

A 混淆 factor 与实施 vehicle；C 把分析工具当保证。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 因子模型揭示跨资产共同风险；经济风险因素与可实施 investment-factor portfolios 要区分。

VCV 的 factor model 是风险估计工具，risk budgeting 用共同风险分配贡献；两者让资产标签下的暴露进入量化配置。

**Boundary：** 因子并非天然可交易，也不保证稳定低相关；实现工具会有剩余风险与成本。
