# Case 2018052801000003

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Young, 47, wants to retire in fifteen years. Current earnings cover expenses. She has $5.5m sale proceeds in money market, $3m tax-deferred retirement assets, $0.5m education and $0.4m unexpected-needs accounts, and an owned home. Childhood financial hardship strongly influences her need for safety; she wants no losses in any account.

For the initial allocation use **only the $5.5m proceeds**. She wants at least 6% after tax with no unnecessary risk. Tax rate is 25%; PE minimum is $500,000.

**Exhibit 1 — Proposals**

| Asset | P1 | P2 | P3 | Pretax return |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Municipal bonds | 5% | 35% | 30% | 3% |
| Small-cap equity | 50% | 10% | 35% | 12% |
| Large-cap equity | 35% | 50% | 35% | 10% |
| PE | 10% | 5% | 0% | 25% |
| After-tax volatility | 28.2% | 16.3% | 15.5% | — |

The source does not explicitly state municipal-bond tax exemption; the analysis identifies the convention and also checks the all-taxable alternative.

Rebalancing alternatives with the source's matching-risk intention are 80% equity ±8% and 75% equity ±10.7% for the taxable proceeds and tax-deferred account.

Ten years later she receives an early-retirement offer continuing salary/benefits for three years. After accepting, she has low immediate liquidity needs and wants additional equity for **unforeseen costs more than a decade away**; the adviser has a positive long-term equity view.
