# Case · Hadpret · Inflation & Policy

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Eastland's leading indicators have fallen for three months, its yield curve is inverted and private borrowing is projected to fall. Hadpret predicts contraction, lower inflation and possible deflation. The portfolio holds cash at cyclical lows, high-quality debt, real estate, and asset-intensive/commodity equities.

Hadpret expects monetary easing and tax cuts financed with long-term government debt. Eastland currently has a credible fixed exchange rate against Northland and unrestricted capital flows. In the exchange-rate question, consider three scenarios independently: capital controls; a floating exchange rate; and weaker confidence that the peg will remain permanent.

<template #prompt>

**Discuss** the implications of Hadpret’s inflation forecast on the expected returns of the fund’s holdings of:
i. cash.
ii. bonds.
iii. equities.
iv. real estate

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

Disinflation/deflation：cash purchasing power、high-quality nominal bonds 较受支持，cyclical equities / property 较承压。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Cash 的 nominal reinvestment rate 可能下降，但 deflation 支持实际购买力。
- High-quality bonds 可因 yields 下降涨价、固定支付实际价值上升。
- Commodity / asset-intensive equities 的 demand、prices、earnings 下降。
- Property rent/occupancy/value 受需求压制；lower rates 可能部分抵消。

### Minimum Passing Answer

Cash gains purchasing power in deflation despite lower nominal yields. High-quality nominal bonds benefit from falling yields. Commodity/asset-intensive equities and real estate face weaker earnings, rents and values; rate relief may partly offset the decline.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 通胀影响购买力、现金流与折现率；unexpected inflation 与已定价预期不同。

通胀不仅进入 CME 的名义回报，还决定 goals-based 的未来支出与养老金负债是否需要 inflation hedging。

**Boundary：** 信用利差和租金恶化可抵消利率下降的收益；通缩不等于所有债券和股票都受益。

- 固定名义债务在通缩下谁受益？
- 现金名义利率下降是否意味着实际购买力下降？

### Exam Takeaway

同时问现金流、折现率和实际购买力，避免只用一个通胀方向判断所有资产。

## Question 2 · Constructed Response

<p class="source-note">Source: Local 原版书 / Core-Asset Allocation-CME_ORIG.md / No.2021052701000003-2 — adapted。</p>

<p class="source-note">原出版年份未确认；参考答案与得分点为推导，仓库无答案键。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2021052701000003-2" type="cr">

<template #prompt>

In response to the projected cyclical decline in the Eastland economy and in private sector borrowing over the next year, Hadpret expects a change in the monetary and fiscal policy mix. He forecasts that the Eastland central bank will ease monetary policy.On the fiscal side, Hadpret expects the Eastland government to enact a substantial tax cut. As a result, Hadpret forecasts large government deficits that will be financed by the issuance of long-term government securities.
**Discuss** the relationship between the shape of the yield curve and the monetary and fiscal policy mix projected by Hadpret.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

Yield curve 倾向 steepen。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Monetary easing 压低 short rates。
- Tax cuts 与 deficit financing 增加长期债券供给，支撑 long rates。
- 复苏/通胀预期也可使 long end 相对 short end 更高；非保证 long yield 必涨。

### Minimum Passing Answer

The curve tends to steepen: easing lowers short rates, while long-term deficit financing and recovery/inflation expectations support long yields relative to short yields.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 政策组合通过短端、债券供给与增长/通胀预期影响曲线；Taylor rule 拆解基准与反应项。

政策先进入 business cycle 判断，再通过 duration 与 risk premiums 进入固定收益预期，不能直接等同于实现回报。

**Boundary：** 按题干定义使用 gap；宽松货币政策与财政扩张通常使曲线变陡，但不保证长债收益率必涨。

### Related Concepts

- [从 Business Cycle 识别政策和资产方向](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/01-cme-part-1/step-03)
- [将 Yield、Risk Premium 与实现回报分开](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/02-cme-part-2/step-01)
- [把国内宏观判断放进开放经济约束](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/01-cme-part-1/step-06)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 如果题目给的是中性名义利率，应否再加通胀？
- 债券发行集中长端，而央行降息，曲线通常怎么变？

### Exam Takeaway

先区分真实/名义和期限，再判断政策组合的影响。

## Question 3 · Constructed Response

<p class="source-note">Source: Local 原版书 / Core-Asset Allocation-CME_ORIG.md / No.2021052701000003-3 — adapted。</p>

<p class="source-note">原出版年份未确认；参考答案与得分点为推导，仓库无答案键。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2021052701000003-3" type="cr">

<template #prompt>

Currently, Eastland’s currency is fixed relative to the currency of the country of Northland, and Eastland maintains policies that allow unrestricted capital flows. Hadpret examines the relationship between interest rates and exchange rates. He considers three possible scenarios for the Eastland economy:
Scenario 1 Shift in policy restricting capital flowsScenario 2 Shift in policy allowing the currency to floatScenario 3 Shift in investor belief toward a lack of full credibility that the exchange rate will be fixed foreverDiscuss how interest rate and exchange rate linkages between Eastland and Northland might change under each scenario.

Note: Consider each scenario independently.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

分别放松资本开放、汇率固定或 peg credibility 条件。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Capital controls 限制套利，国内利率可更多偏离 anchor rates。
- Floating currency 增加 monetary independence，利差含 FX expectations / risk premium。
- Weak peg credibility 产生 expected-depreciation / risk compensation，domestic rates 可更高。

### Minimum Passing Answer

With capital controls, interest rates can diverge more. With a float, monetary autonomy increases and currency expectations enter the linkage. With a less credible peg, expected depreciation and risk premiums can raise domestic rates. Treat each scenario independently.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 财政余额与私人储蓄投资共同决定外部余额；汇率制度约束货币政策自主性。

开放经济的利率与资本流动框架是 currency CME 的起点，也解释跨国股票与债券风险为何会共同变化。

**Boundary：** 身份关系需要题干保持其他项不变；固定汇率的利率联系需要资本开放且制度可信。

### Related Concepts

- [区分 PPP 长期锚与 Capital Flows 短期力量](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/02-cme-part-2/step-04)
- [把 Monetary / Fiscal Policy 转成利率判断](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/01-cme-part-1/step-05)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 私人净储蓄同时上升时，财政赤字扩大一定令 NX 降低吗？
- 固定汇率失去可信度时为什么会出现利差？

### Exam Takeaway

开放经济判断先锁定资本流动、汇率制度和题干保持不变的变量。
