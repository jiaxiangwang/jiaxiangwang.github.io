# Case · Cambo · Growth & Cycle

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Cambo forecasts 9.0% long-term equity return with stable profit share. Her annual inputs are labor growth 0.5%, productivity growth 1.3%, inflation 2.2% and dividend yield 2.8%. Some return is attributed to P/E change.

The economist expects **late expansion** over the next twelve months. Cambo forecasts with a time-series model using a composite leading-indicator index; the economist uses a detailed econometric model.

<template #prompt>

**Calculate** the implied contribution to Cambo’s US equity return forecast from the expected change in the P/E.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

Implied P/E contribution = 2.20 percentage points。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Real trend growth=0.5+1.3=1.8%。
- Nominal earnings growth≈1.8+2.2=4.0%；assume stable profit share / share count。
- 9.0−2.8−4.0=2.2%。

### Minimum Passing Answer

P/E contribution = 9.0% − 2.8% − (0.5% + 1.3% + 2.2%) = 2.2 percentage points, with no dilution assumed in this aggregate-return decomposition.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 真实趋势增长近似为劳动投入增长加生产率增长；长期盈利需要经济支持。

把趋势增长接到 Grinold–Kroner，可检验回报究竟来自经营增长、股息还是估值变化。

**Boundary：** 趋势锚不是逐年预测；利润份额与股份数量变动会改变每股收益。

- [从 Business Cycle 识别政策和资产方向](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/01-cme-part-1/step-03)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 利润占 GDP 比例短期上升时，能否把盈利增速机械锁在 GDP？
- 如果 P/E 不变，给定增长和股息后预期回报是多少？

### Exam Takeaway

长期预期必须能持续，不能永远依赖利润份额或估值倍数上升。

## Question 2 · Constructed Response

<p class="source-note">Source: Local 原版书 / Core-Asset Allocation-CME_ORIG.md / No.2021052701000002-2 — adapted。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2021052701000002-2" type="cr">

<template #prompt>

At the investment committee meeting, the firm’s chief economist predicts that the economy will enter the late expansion phase of the business cycle in the next 12 months.
**Discuss**, based on the chief economist’s prediction, the implications for the following:
i. Bond yields ii. Equity returns iii. Short-term interest rates

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

Late expansion：bond yields / short rates 趋升；equity 仍有盈利支持但上行更脆弱。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- 产能/通胀压力促使 tightening，short rates 较高且 rising。
- Bond yields 面临上升压力、prices 下跌风险。
- Equity earnings 仍增长，但 margin、discount-rate 与周期转向风险降低回报支撑。

### Minimum Passing Answer

Late expansion raises inflation/tightening pressure: short rates and bond yields tend to rise. Equities may retain earnings support but become vulnerable to tighter policy and a slowing cycle.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 周期通过盈利、通胀和政策改变回报；市场会提前反映下一阶段。

周期预测为 TAA 提供条件性观点，并通过收益率与信用溢价进入债券回报预测。

**Boundary：** 供给冲击、政策时滞和已定价预期，会打破简单的阶段—资产机械关系。

### Related Concepts

- [把 Monetary / Fiscal Policy 转成利率判断](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/01-cme-part-1/step-05)
- [将 Yield、Risk Premium 与实现回报分开](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/02-cme-part-2/step-01)
- [将 Tactical Views 放在 SAA 与 IPS 边界内](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/05-constraints/step-05)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 曲线倒挂后若开始降息，曲线可能如何变化？
- 若通胀来自供给冲击，股票和债券是否同时受压？

### Exam Takeaway

先识别阶段，再检查政策反应与市场是否已提前定价。

## Question 3 · Constructed Response

<p class="source-note">Source: Local 原版书 / Core-Asset Allocation-CME_ORIG.md / No.2021052701000002-3 — adapted。</p>

<p class="source-note">原出版年份未确认；参考答案与得分点为推导，仓库无答案键。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2021052701000002-3" type="cr">

<template #prompt>

Cambo compares her business cycle forecasting approach to the approach used by the chief economist. Cambo bases her equity market forecast on a time-series model using a composite index of leading indicators as the key input, whereas the chief economist uses a detailed econometric model to generate his economic forecasts.
**Discuss** strengths and weaknesses of the economic forecasting approaches used by Cambo and the chief economist.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

Leading indicators 简洁及时但可能假信号；econometric model 逻辑一致、能模拟政策，但复杂且可能 false precision。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Cambo：leading composite 易解释、更新快；关系改变会误报/漏报，历史领先并非永久。
- Chief economist：consistent structural relationships / scenarios；模型设定、参数和数据误差、维护负担。

### Minimum Passing Answer

Leading indicators are timely and simple but can give false signals or lose stability. Econometric models impose consistency and support scenarios, but are complex and vulnerable to specification/input error and false precision.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 预测流程先明确资产与期限，最后检验并反馈；显著性要有经济机制支持。

CME 的估计误差会在 MVO 中被放大；使用稳健配置方法之前，先处理输入本身的质量。

**Boundary：** 长样本并不自动更可靠；制度改变会使历史平均失去代表性。

### Related Concepts

- [减少 MVO 输入误差造成的极端权重](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/04-principles/step-02)
- [让 Variance–Covariance Matrix 反映真实风险](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/02-cme-part-2/step-05)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 如果加入更早的低通胀制度样本，预测一定更好吗？
- 若指标只是同期相关，能否用于 TAA？

### Exam Takeaway

先检查数据与机制，再把统计结果当作配置输入。
