# Case · Lennon · Wealth & Goals

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

**Exhibit 1 — Lennon: economic assets, obligations and goals**

Lennon, age 56, will retire in four years. Her employment-income PV is $4.5m. She owns an $8m investment portfolio, $1m employer shares restricted from sale for two years, a $2m unencumbered home, a $3m vacation home with $1m mortgage, and a $2.5m vested defined-contribution pension.

Goals/obligations (PV): Everett's education/future practice $1.5m; Marshall's essential special-needs trust $2m within a year; a university endowed chair $1.75m in two years; future consumption $9m. She sells winners and retains losers. Goal sub-portfolios are being considered.

Candidates discuss institutions:

- Trainor says goals-based is only appropriate for individuals; institutions must focus on assets or liabilities.
- Kelly mentions a Sharpe/volatility objective and law-of-large-numbers liability modeling, and separately says institutions can segment general-account assets by business-line objectives.

On asset classes, Kelly favors high within-class/low across-class correlations and claims every class needs high liquidity/low costs for rebalancing. Trainor stresses low pairwise correlations. Other comments say: EM cannot be a separate class; asset classes offer non-skill-based ex ante premiums; and classes must have no overlapping risk sources.

C. 5.75.

### Why?

**Boundary：** 受限捐款不能挪用，future inflows 不是当前现金；非交易 human capital 应作为既有暴露而非自由调权工具。

<template #prompt>

Which of the sub-portfolios dedicated to Lennon’s aspirational goals is in the **best** position to tolerate the greatest risk exposure? The one dedicated to:

</template>

<template #a>

Marshall’s trust

</template>

<template #b>

Everett’s education

</template>

<template #c>

University endowment

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. University endowment

### Why?

大学 endowment 是较可调整的 aspirational goal；Marshall 的终身保障与教育更重要且期限/确定性要求更紧。

### Distractor Analysis

A 核心 special-needs trust 要高保障；B 教育更近且高优先级。最高可承受风险不只按 horizon。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 按期限和成功概率选 maximum minimum return，再以其折现目标金额，汇总资金与配置。

目标框架来自 risk definition；inflation 形成未来支出，simulation 检查多期及联合 shortfall，taxes 决定净回报口径。

**Boundary：** 期初/期末、nominal/real 与每目标/联合成功概率不能混淆；正态近似不能覆盖提款路径风险。

<template #prompt>

The **most appropriate** statement in regards to approaches to asset allocation by institutions is made by:

</template>

<template #a>

Trainor.

</template>

<template #b>

Kelly, regarding their goals-based allocations.

</template>

<template #c>

Kelly, regarding the Sharpe ratio and modeling of liabilities.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. Kelly, regarding their goals-based allocations.

### Why?

某些保险业务将 general account 按业务线分 sub-portfolios，有类似 goals-based segmentation，Kelly 这部分合适。

### Distractor Analysis

A 机构并非只能 asset-only/liability；C 把 Sharpe objective 与 liability modeling 拼接成普遍机构目标，无法替代真实风险定义。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** Asset-only 看资产风险效率，liability-relative 看相对支付义务，goals-based 看目标 shortfall。

同一组 CME 可进入不同配置目标函数；Principles 中的方法选择必须继承这里的风险定义，而不能倒过来让工具决定目标。

**Boundary：** 机构也可以采用目标分层；低资产波动并不保证低 funding risk。

## Question 4 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local Other / Core-Asset Allocation-AA_OTH.md / No.2025072102000040-4 — adapted。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000040-4" answer="C">

<template #prompt>

In the candidates’ responses to Fox regarding the relevant characteristics of asset classes, the statement that is **least accurate** is:

</template>

<template #a>

Trainor’s.

</template>

<template #b>

Kelly’s regarding correlations.

</template>

<template #c>

Kelly’s regarding rebalancing.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. Kelly’s regarding rebalancing.

### Why?

Kelly 要求所有 asset classes 有高 liquidity/low costs，排除了有效 illiquid classes，过度绝对。

### Distractor Analysis

A 低 pairwise correlations 是分散价值线索；B 同类高、类间较低是合理分类逻辑，不意味着永远固定。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 同类同质、类间有分散价值、holdings 避免重叠，并具实施容量；不要求风险因子零重叠。

资产类只是对风险的第一层表达；factor analysis 识别跨类共同暴露，real-world liquidity 与 size 决定其可实施性。

**Boundary：** 低相关、精细名称或漂亮指数都不保证经济分散；illiquid 类不因成本高就自动失去资格。
