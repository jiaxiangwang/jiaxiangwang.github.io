# Case · Wakuluk · Macro CME

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Wakuluk prepares CME for global portfolios. She began her career amid sharp volatility and poor returns, and now uses objective evidence. Her forecasting method combines a structural model and a diffusion index; past success gives her high confidence.

**Exhibit 1 — Current cycle forecast**

| Country | Market type | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | Developed | Initial recovery |
| Y | Developed | Contraction |
| Z | Developing | Late upswing |

She uses historical growth as the starting trend estimate and assumes short rates move with expected inflation and are procyclical.

For Y, assess each observation separately:

1. Monetary policy is persistently loose; fiscal policy is tight.
2. Transfer payments will rise substantially and taxation will become more progressive.
3. The yield curve signals slowdown, with bond yields already reflecting contractionary conditions.

## Question 3 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local 原版书 / Core-Asset Allocation-CME_ORIG.md / No.2020012102000001-3 — adapted。</p>

<p class="source-note">原出版年份未确认；参考答案与得分点为推导，仓库无答案键。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2020012102000001-3" answer="B">

<template #prompt>

Wakuluk is **most likely** to make signifcant adjustments to her estimate of the future growth trend for which of the following countries?

</template>

<template #a>

Country Y only

</template>

<template #b>

Country Z only

</template>

<template #c>

Countries Y and Z

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. Country Z only

### Why?

发展中 Country Z 的经济结构、追赶与制度变化可能使历史 trend 更需调整；成熟 X/Y 相对稳定。

### Distractor Analysis

A/C 将当前 contraction 与长期趋势需大幅修订混为一谈。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 真实趋势增长近似为劳动投入增长加生产率增长；长期盈利需要经济支持。

把趋势增长接到 Grinold–Kroner，可检验回报究竟来自经营增长、股息还是估值变化。

**Boundary：** 趋势锚不是逐年预测；利润份额与股份数量变动会改变每股收益。

### Related Concepts

- [从盈利、分红与估值拆解 Equity Return](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/02-cme-part-2/step-02)
- [从 Business Cycle 识别政策和资产方向](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/01-cme-part-1/step-03)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 利润占 GDP 比例短期上升时，能否把盈利增速机械锁在 GDP？
- 如果 P/E 不变，给定增长和股息后预期回报是多少？

### Exam Takeaway

长期预期必须能持续，不能永远依赖利润份额或估值倍数上升。

<CfaQuestion id="2020012102000001-4" answer="A">

<template #prompt>

Based on Exhibit 1 and Wakuluk’s assumptions about short-term rates and expected inﬂation, short-term rates in Country X are **most likely** to be:

</template>

<template #a>

low and bottoming.

</template>

<template #b>

above average and rising.

</template>

<template #c>

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. low and bottoming.

### Why?

Initial recovery 时政策仍宽松，通胀压力低，short rates 通常 low and bottoming。

### Distractor Analysis

B 接近较后扩张；C 接近 slowdown。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 周期通过盈利、通胀和政策改变回报；市场会提前反映下一阶段。

周期预测为 TAA 提供条件性观点，并通过收益率与信用溢价进入债券回报预测。

**Boundary：** 供给冲击、政策时滞和已定价预期，会打破简单的阶段—资产机械关系。

先识别阶段，再检查政策反应与市场是否已提前定价。

## Question 6 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local 原版书 / Core-Asset Allocation-CME_ORIG.md / No.2020012102000001-6 — adapted。</p>

<p class="source-note">原出版年份未确认；参考答案与得分点为推导，仓库无答案键。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2020012102000001-6" answer="A">

<template #prompt>

Based on Observation 1, fiscal and monetary policies in Country Y will **most likely** lead to:

</template>

<template #a>

low nominal rates.

</template>

<template #b>

high nominal rates.

</template>

<template #c>

either high or low nominal rates.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. low nominal rates.

### Why?

Loose monetary + tight fiscal 的组合，短端受宽松压低，政府借款/需求压力有限，典型 nominal rates 较低。

### Distractor Analysis

B 是相反 mix 的倾向；C 放弃了题干已指定的经典组合。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 政策组合通过短端、债券供给与增长/通胀预期影响曲线；Taylor rule 拆解基准与反应项。

政策先进入 business cycle 判断，再通过 duration 与 risk premiums 进入固定收益预期，不能直接等同于实现回报。

**Boundary：** 按题干定义使用 gap；宽松货币政策与财政扩张通常使曲线变陡，但不保证长债收益率必涨。

- [从 Business Cycle 识别政策和资产方向](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/01-cme-part-1/step-03)

- [把国内宏观判断放进开放经济约束](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/01-cme-part-1/step-06)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 如果题目给的是中性名义利率，应否再加通胀？
- 债券发行集中长端，而央行降息，曲线通常怎么变？

### Exam Takeaway

先区分真实/名义和期限，再判断政策组合的影响。
