The marginal contribution to total risk for an asset class represents:

</template>

<template #a>

the total amount of portfolio risk contributed by the asset class.

</template>

<template #b>

the rate of change in portfolio risk for a small change in the asset class weight.

</template>

<template #c>

the required change in asset class weights to maintain a target portfolio risk level.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. the rate of change in portfolio risk for a small change in the asset class weight.

### Why?

MCTR 是 weight 小幅增加对 portfolio volatility 的边际影响。

### Distractor Analysis

A 是 absolute contribution 的描述；C 不是 MCTR 的定义。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** MCTR 是边际风险，ACTR 是权重乘边际风险；risk parity 平分贡献而非本金。

Factor/VCV 决定 MCTR；MVO 的最优条件解释为何 marginal risk 的回报应一致，但现实约束与 liabilities 会改变预算。

**Boundary：** 相等比例条件限于适用的 interior optimum；risk parity 也不能仅凭低收益率推资本权重。
