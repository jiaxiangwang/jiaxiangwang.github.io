A model portfolio has an expected return of 9% with 15% expected volatility. An individual wants an 85% chance to meet his investment goal in five years' time. When using the goal-based asset allocation approach, the relevant discount rate should be:

</template>

<template #a>

lower than 9%.

</template>

<template #b>

equal to 9%.

</template>

<template #c>

higher than 9%.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. lower than 9%.

### Why?

85% success rate 高于均值对应的中位位置，在题目简化分布下要用 lower-tail return，低于 9%。

### Distractor Analysis

B/C 低估达到高成功率的所需资金。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 按期限和成功概率选 maximum minimum return，再以其折现目标金额，汇总资金与配置。

目标框架来自 risk definition；inflation 形成未来支出，simulation 检查多期及联合 shortfall，taxes 决定净回报口径。

**Boundary：** 期初/期末、nominal/real 与每目标/联合成功概率不能混淆；正态近似不能覆盖提款路径风险。
