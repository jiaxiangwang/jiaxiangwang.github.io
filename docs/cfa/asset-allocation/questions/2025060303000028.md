<template #prompt>

An investor notes that a country's domestic currency has appreciated after the country opened its financial markets to foreign investment and reported strong economic growth. According to the overshooting mechanism theory, over the long-run, the domestic currency will **most likely**:

</template>

<template #a>

depreciate.

</template>

<template #b>

stabilize at its current level.

</template>

<template #c>

continue to appreciate.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. depreciate.

### Why?

题干指定 overshooting：快速流入将汇率推过新的长期均衡，随后从 overshot level 回落。

### Distractor Analysis

B/C 忽略过度反应后的调整；不是断言长期汇率必须回到开放前原水平。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** PPP 以相对通胀提供长期锚，资本流动可主导短期；先检查报价方向。

国际政策制度接到货币预期，再进入外币资产的本币回报；资本突然撤出也会改变 emerging debt 和 liquidity 风险。

**Boundary：** 利差可能补偿贬值或风险；PPP 偏离可以持续，短期 capital flows 并非长期购买力证明。
