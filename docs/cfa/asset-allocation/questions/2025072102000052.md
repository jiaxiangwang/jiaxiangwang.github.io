## Vignette & Exhibits

**Titan:** $10m endowment, mandatory annual spending 3% of beginning assets. Enrollment falls and sanctions are expected to reduce donations. It proposes replacing 10% liquid bonds with PE.

| Fund | Current | Proposed | Vehicle AUM | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Global equity | 70% | 70% | $25bn | $0.5m |
| IG bonds | 27% | 17% | $50bn | $0.25m |
| Sun-Fin PE | 0% | 10% | $0.4bn | $1m |
| Cash | 3% | 3% | $50bn | $0.1m |

**Fordhart:** $2bn endowment, same 3% annual spending rule. Enrollment/tuition grow; a settlement removes an annual $50m operating obligation.

| Fund | Current | Proposed | Vehicle AUM | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large-cap equity | 49% | 29% | $50bn | $0.25m |
| IG bonds | 49% | 59% | $80bn | $0.5m |
| CFQ PE | 0% | 10% | $0.5bn | $5m |
| Cash | 2% | 2% | $50bn | $0.25m |

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

Titan：liquidity 不足与单基金最低额导致集中。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Enrollment/donations 下降提高对 fund 支持的需求；仍 mandatory3%spend。
- 把10%从 bonds 换 PE 降低 liquid funding capacity。
- 1mminimum 占10m 账户10%，限制分散并承担 capital-call/lockup 风险。

### Minimum Passing Answer

Falling tuition and donations raise funding/liquidity needs, yet the proposal shifts liquid bonds into locked PE. The $1m minimum is 10% of Titan, producing concentrated exposure and limiting diversification.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** target weight 乘 AUM 才是实际投资；小账户受最低额与可达性，大账户受容量与治理资源。

可行机会集先限制 MVO，再与 liquidity budget 和 IPS 授权结合；资产规模不改变目标本身，却改变实施方式。

**Boundary：** 能满足 minimum ticket 不等于风险、费用或流动性合适；所有方案必须服从题干法律与政策限制。
