<template #prompt>

An emerging market has a growing fiscal deficit, weak corporate governance and an authoritarian anti-investor government. Relative to a debt investor, the equity investor **most likely** has greater exposure to:

</template>

<template #a>

the growing fiscal deficit.

</template>

<template #b>

the weak corporate governance.

</template>

<template #c>

the authoritarian and anti-investor government.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. the weak corporate governance.

### Why?

股权为 residual claim，弱治理可使控制股东侵占利益、信息质量降低，equity investors 更直接暴露。

### Distractor Analysis

A 的 fiscal risk 与 C 的政治风险也影响 debt investors，不能作为股票相对债券额外风险的最直接区别。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** G–K 拆分收入、增长、股本与估值；Singer–Terhaar 根据市场整合程度区分系统风险与总风险补偿。

长期盈利输入接自 trend growth；全球整合和治理风险又影响 robust MVO 的预期与相关性假设。

**Boundary：** 整合过渡的估值增益不等于之后的均衡回报；required premium 本身不能证明资产便宜。
