# Case · Trunch Foundation · Property & Bonds

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Trunch Foundation reviews property and bond CME, and possible changes within SAA policy ranges.

The property manager says:

1. Adding traded REITs should substantially diversify an equity portfolio **over the next year**.
2. Over multiple years, REITs are more correlated with direct property and less with equities.

**Exhibit 1 — Long-run property inputs:** cap rate 4.70%; GDP growth 4.60%.

The private-property model treats observed returns as an average of current true return and lagged observed return, using REIT proxies for the unobservable components.

**Exhibit 2 — Domestic sovereign portfolio:** Macaulay duration 3.00; modified duration 2.94; YTM 2.00%. A single 20 bp yield increase is expected soon, then stable yields for three years.

Country XYZ is a developed sovereign with an upward curve and a 100 bp 2-year/10-year yield spread. Domestic company ZMC was downgraded two rating steps; short rates are expected to rise and the sovereign/next-highest-quality agency spread widened 10 bp.

**Exhibit 3 — Emerging-market screening**

| Input | Republic A | Republic B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fiscal deficit/GDP | 6.50% | 8.20% |
| Debt/GDP | 90.10% | 104.20% |
| Current-account deficit/GDP | 5.20% | 7.10% |
| FX reserves / short-term debt | 90.30% | 70.10% |

Both economies rely substantially on mineral exports. A separate developed-market issuer, Xdelp, benefits from synchronized mid-cycle growth, stable government policies, rising current-account surplus, an appreciating currency and an expected credit upgrade.

**Exhibit 4 — Policy ranges**

| Asset | Minimum | Maximum | Actual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic fixed income | 40% | 80% | 43.22% |
| International fixed income | 5% | 10% | 6.17% |
| Emerging-market fixed income | 0% | 2% | 0% |
| Real estate | 2% | 6% | 3.34% |

### Correct Answer

B. Only Statement 2

### Why?

### Distractor Analysis

A 高估短期分散化；C 包含不可靠 Statement 1。

**Core：** 收入收益与 NOI 增长支持回报，cap rate 的相对变化产生重估；appraisal smoothing 压低观察风险。

**Core：** 要求收益由多个 premium 构成；实现回报取决于价格与再投资，YTM 不是任意 horizon 的保证。

**Boundary：** duration-horizon 抵消需要简化利率变化与再投资条件；曲线扭曲、违约与交易成本会破坏近似。
