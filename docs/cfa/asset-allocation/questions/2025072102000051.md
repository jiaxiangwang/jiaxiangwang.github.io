# Case · KUE & Koval · Constraints & Tax

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

KUE has rising tuition enrollment, little need for endowment support over the next five years, and a lower spending rate.

**Exhibit 1 — IPS and actual weights**

| Class | Actual | Target | Lower | Upper |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Developed equity | 30% | 30% | 25% | 35% |
| EM equity | 28% | 30% | 25% | 35% |
| IG bonds | 15% | 20% | 15% | 25% |
| Private property | 15% | 10% | 5% | 15% |
| Infrastructure | 12% | 10% | 5% | 15% |

Capara says (1) Sharpe can evaluate TAA relative to SAA; (2) discretionary TAA captures persistent return anomalies; (3) higher expected risk-adjusted return permits exceeding IPS limits.

**Exhibit 2 — Excess-return forecasts:** developed equity 2%, EM 5%, IG bonds −3%, property 3%, infrastructure −1%. The allocation-choice subquestion is excluded because its forecast-preferred answer breaches the actual-weight limit.

Koval's taxable account equally holds HY bonds, IG bonds and long-term-capital-gains equity. Interest is taxed more heavily than dividends/long-term gains; losses can offset gains. He rejects EM because a vivid recent crisis caused losses in his family trust. Mayer proposes pretax and after-tax MVO comparisons.

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000051-1" answer="A">

<template #prompt>

The change in the annual spending rate, in conjunction with the board’s expectations regarding future enrollment and the need for endowment support, could justify that KUE’s target weight for:

</template>

<template #a>

infrastructure be increased.

</template>

<template #b>

investment-grade bonds be increased.

</template>

<template #c>

private real estate equity be decreased.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. infrastructure be increased.

### Why?

支出率下降、tuition 增长让 liquidity needs 较低，可增加 long-term illiquid infrastructure，仍检查 calls。

### Distractor Analysis

B 增加 bonds 不是此需求变化最自然方向；C 降低 private property 与新增 risk capacity 不一致。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 可锁定资本取决于提款、calls、inflows 和压力缓冲；private index 不等于 actual vehicle。

Appraisal smoothing 属于 CME 输入问题，private commitments 属于执行问题；Monte Carlo 应把 calls 和 inflow shortfalls 放入路径。

**Boundary：** 长期 horizon 不保证 liquidity；稳定 inflows 和低支出须经压力检查，premium 不是实现收益保证。

- [用 Asset Size、Capacity 与 Regulation 划定可行配置](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/05-constraints/step-01)

### CFA Could Also Ask

- 捐赠和 tuition 同时下降，为何会双重恶化 liquidity？
- Private asset index 的低波动是否代表可即时退出？

### Exam Takeaway

先支付与现金缓冲，再争取 illiquidity premium。

<template #prompt>

Which of Capara’s statements regarding tactical asset allocation is **correct**?

</template>

<template #a>

Statement 1

</template>

<template #b>

Statement 2

</template>

<template #c>

Statement 3

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. Statement 1

### Why?

同口径 Sharpe 可比较 TAA 和 SAA 的 risk-adjusted outcome，Statement1合适。

### Distractor Analysis

B 的 return anomaly-rule 描述更贴 systematic 而非 discretionary；C 不能因 expected alpha 越 IPSlimits。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** TAA 在 IPS 内利用短期观点；策略比较以 SAA 为基准并扣额外实施成本。

CME 提供战术输入，governance 限定授权，liquidity/tax 决定实施代价；rebalancing 是无战术观点时的风险恢复机制。

**Boundary：** 更高 alpha 不允许越界；持久长期环境改变可能需要 SAA review，而不是永久 tactical tilt。

## Question 4 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local Other / Core-Asset Allocation-AA_OTH.md / No.2025072102000051-4 — adapted。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000051-4" answer="C">

<template #prompt>

Given Koval’s current portfolio and the tax laws of the country in which he lives, Koval’s portfolio would be more tax efficient if he reallocated his taxable account to hold more:

</template>

<template #a>

high-yield bonds.

</template>

<template #b>

investment-grade bonds.

</template>

<template #c>

domestic equities focused on long-term capital gain opportunities.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. domestic equities focused on long-term capital gain opportunities.

### Why?

税法题设 interest 较高、long-termgains 税较低且可递延，capital-gains-oriented domestic equity 更 taxefficient。

### Distractor Analysis

A/B 都增加 interest-heavyexposure；不拿题设税率推到所有国家。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 按 after-tax 可消费回报与风险配置；asset location 优先把税负较重 exposures 放在适合账户。

Tax 改变 MVO 输入、goal funding 和 rebalancing cost；整体经济风险要跨账户合并，而不能按税前名义余额直接加权。

**Boundary：** correlation 不变仅在正的固定线性缩放下成立；progressive rates、gains deferral、loss offsets 需另建模。

- [用成本与风险偏离确定 Rebalancing Policy](/cfa/asset-allocation/review/03-overview/step-06)

- 低周转资本利得资产与高周转策略谁优先放税优账户？

### Exam Takeaway

净回报、净风险、账户位置与交易税成本要一致。

## Question 5 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local Other / Core-Asset Allocation-AA_OTH.md / No.2025072102000051-5 — adapted。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2025072102000051-5" answer="B">

<template #prompt>

Koval’s attitude toward emerging markets equity reflects which of the following behavioral biases?

</template>

<template #a>

Hindsight bias

</template>

<template #b>

Availability bias

</template>

<template #c>

Illusion of control

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. Availability bias

### Why?

对 recentcrisis 损失的鲜明记忆驱动一概拒绝 EM，是 availability。

### Distractor Analysis

A 需声称事后早知道；C 需误以为能控制市场。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 用具体行为识别 bias，并说明其对预测、配置或执行的影响。

Bias 可以污染 CME 或阻止 rebalancing；goals-based 与书面治理规则帮助执行，但仍要检查经济资产负债表的总体暴露。

**Boundary：** 目标分层可以合理；认知纠偏与情绪适配不同，不能用同一教育话术处理所有客户。

- 因一次鲜明市场亏损拒绝 EM 是 availability 还是 representativeness？
- 目标账户划分何时改善纪律，何时隐藏风险？

### Exam Takeaway

先引用行为证据，再写偏差与配置后果。
