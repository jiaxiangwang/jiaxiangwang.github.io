Johansson recommends a hedging/return-seeking approach for a pension with assets $10bn and liability PV $8.5bn. Liability returns are driven by index-linked government bonds.

| Allocation | Cash | Index-linked government | Corporate bonds | Equity | Mean | Volatility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 15% | 70% | 0% | 15% | 3.4% | 7.0% |
| 2 | 5% | 15% | 30% | 50% | 6.2% | 12.0% |
| 3 | 0% | 85% | 5% | 10% | 3.6% | 8.5% |

**Determine** the **most appropriate** allocation and justify it.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Conclusion

Allocation3最适合 hedging/return-seeking。

### Scoring Points

以下为依据解题逻辑推导的可得分点，不是官方评分表。

- Liabilities8.5/Assets10=85%，index-linkedbonds 可在题设下 replicate liability risk。
- Allocation3有85%该债券；余15%以 corporate5%/equity10%return-seeking。
- 其他 mix 不能先覆盖相同 hedgeamount；highestmean 不是目标。

### Minimum Passing Answer

Choose Allocation 3: 85% in index-linked government bonds matches the $8.5bn liabilities within $10bn assets; the remaining 15% is return-seeking, assuming the bond portfolio replicates liability risks.

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 资产相对支付义务的风险决定方法；surplus 与 funding ratio 反映不同信息。

经济资产负债表同时识别 sponsor 经营风险；CME 的 rates/inflation 进入负债价值，governance 决定目标优先级和贡献政策。

**Boundary：** 负债估值与 cash-flow 风险不能用 asset Sharpe 替代；等额资产负债变化不保持 ratio，closed 不等于 frozen。
