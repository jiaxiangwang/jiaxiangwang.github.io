<template #prompt>

An advisor is using mean–variance optimization to guide his advice to an investor. If the advisor expresses returns and variances in decimal form, the utility of the investor's asset mix is **best** expressed as the expected return minus:

</template>

<template #a>

the expected variance of the asset mix divided by the investor's risk aversion coefficient.

</template>

<template #b>

the expected standard deviation of the asset mix scaled by the investor's risk aversion coefficient.

</template>

<template #c>

one-half of the expected variance of the asset mix scaled by the investor's risk aversion coefficient.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. one-half of the expected variance of the asset mix scaled by the investor's risk aversion coefficient.

### Why?

小数口径 U=E(R)−0.5λσ²；惩罚的是 variance，由 risk-aversion coefficient 加权。

### Distractor Analysis

A 将 λ 放到分母；B 把 standard deviation 当 variance。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** MVO 建有效边界，risk aversion 选 utility；加入无风险资产后比较同目标下的混合风险。

输入来自 CME，但 expected return 的小误差会被权重优化放大；下一步的 robust methods 与 simulation 分别处理输入和路径问题。

**Boundary：** 百分数与小数的 utility 系数不同；经典 tangency 结论依赖借贷与约束假设。
