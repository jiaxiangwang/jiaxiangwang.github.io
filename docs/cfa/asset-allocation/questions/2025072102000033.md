An investor has decided to retire several years earlier than expected because he has just been offered an early-retirement package by his employer. The investor's financial advisor has suggested a review of his existing asset allocation. The reason for this review is **most likely** classified as a change in:

</template>

<template #a>

</template>

<template #b>

beliefs.

</template>

<template #c>

constraints.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. constraints.

### Why?

外部 early-retirement package 提前结束工作收入，缩短 accumulative horizon 并提前支出，是 constraints 的直接变化。

### Distractor Analysis

A 更对应主动目标修改；B 是市场长期预期变化。一个事件也可能影响多类，但问最直接 trigger。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** SAA review 区分 goals、constraints、long-term beliefs；事件分类依题干驱动，不依固定标签。

Horizon 改变 human capital、目标 PV 与 sequence risk；需要重新检查 goals-based 和 liability funding，而非只移动比例。

**Boundary：** 一个事件能影响多项，问 primary reason 时抓最直接证据；远期 contingency goal 与即时 emergency cash 不同。
