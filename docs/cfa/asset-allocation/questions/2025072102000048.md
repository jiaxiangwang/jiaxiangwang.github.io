## Vignette & Exhibits

The Armstrongs have $8m and three goals:

1. $5m vacation home in five years; success 85%.
2. Current annual spending $100,000 for ten years; success 99%, inflation 3% from Year 2 onward. **The source does not explicitly state whether the first payment is immediate or at year-end.** The analysis provides both timing conventions.
3. $10m nominal donation in twenty-five years; success 75%.

Excess funds go to Module A.

**Exhibit 1 — Annualized minimum expectation returns**

| Horizon / success | A | B | C | D |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 years / 99% | 0.3% | −0.1% | −0.7% | −1.3% |
| 5 years / 85% | 3.7% | 4.3% | 4.4% | 4.3% |
| 5 years / 75% | 4.7% | 5.6% | 5.8% | 5.9% |
| 10 years / 99% | 2.1% | 2.2% | 2.0% | 1.7% |
| 10 years / 85% | 4.5% | 5.4% | 5.6% | 5.6% |
| 10 years / 75% | 5.2% | 6.3% | 6.6% | 6.7% |
| 25 years / 99% | 3.7% | 4.3% | 4.4% | 4.3% |
| 25 years / 85% | 5.3% | 6.3% | 6.7% | 6.8% |
| 25 years / 75% | 5.7% | 6.9% | 7.3% | 7.5% |

Means/volatilities: A 6.5%/6.0%; B 7.9%/7.7%; C 8.5%/8.8%; D 8.8%/9.7%.
