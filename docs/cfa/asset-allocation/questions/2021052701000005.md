# Case · Bader · VCV, Equities & FX

[Question Bank](/cfa/asset-allocation/questions/) · [Source & LOS 核验说明](/cfa/asset-allocation/sources)

## Vignette & Exhibits

Bader estimates a VCV for twenty asset classes with ten years of monthly data. She combines sample and factor-model results using shrinkage. Her assistant says:

1. Shrinkage decreases estimation efficiency versus sample VCV.
2. Sample VCV is biased and inconsistent, so the proposed method is unreliable.
3. Redundant factors or returns entirely determined by common factors can create portfolios that erroneously appear riskless.

For Singer–Terhaar analysis, A remains highly integrated, B becomes more segmented and C becomes more integrated. For A's G–K model she assumes long-run earnings growth of 4% (nominal GDP growth), net buybacks of 2% and perpetual P/E expansion of 0.5% a year.

B has declining per-capita income, a persistent current-account deficit below 2% of GDP and a transition toward IFRS within two years.

**Exhibit 1 — Currency assumptions**

| Input | A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current-account history | Deficit 5% of GDP | Deficit 2% | Surplus 2% |
| Secular trend | Deficit rises | Deficit narrows | Surplus rises |
| Inflation relative to global | Rises | Keeps pace | Falls |
| Capital flow | Steady inflows | Hot money out | Hot money in |
| Predominant holdings | Private equity | Public equity | Public debt |

</template>

<template #a>

Statement 1

</template>

<template #b>

Statement 2

</template>

<template #c>

Statement 3

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. Statement 3

### Why?

冗余因子或忽略 residual risk 可让 factor VCV 出现被错误视为 riskless 的组合，Statement 3 合理。

### Distractor Analysis

A shrinkage 旨在降低噪声/提高估计效率，并非必然降低；B sample VCV 不可无条件称 biased/inconsistent。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** VCV 捕捉共同风险；sample、factor 和 shrinkage 的取舍是噪声与结构的取舍。

风险估计接到 MVO、MCTR 和 simulation；appraisal smoothing 在输入端就会把非流动资产看起来过度有吸引力。

**Boundary：** 静态 VCV 不能包办 regime change、tail risk 与 liquidity；factor model 要保留适当 residual risk。

<CfaQuestion id="2021052701000005-2" answer="B">

<template #prompt>

Based on expectations for changes in integration with the global market, all else being equal, the Singer–Terhaar model implies that Bader should shift capital from Country A to:

</template>

<template #a>

only Country B.

</template>

<template #b>

only Country C.

</template>

<template #c>

both Countries B and C.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. only Country C.

### Why?

C 更 fully integrated，要求风险补偿通常下降，可在整合过渡期获得 valuation gain；B 更分割则反向。此题推导关注 transition revaluation。

### Distractor Analysis

A/C 将更高 segmentation premium 当短期涨价；降低 long-run required return 与过渡 capital gain 方向不同。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** G–K 拆分收入、增长、股本与估值；Singer–Terhaar 根据市场整合程度区分系统风险与总风险补偿。

长期盈利输入接自 trend growth；全球整合和治理风险又影响 robust MVO 的预期与相关性假设。

**Boundary：** 整合过渡的估值增益不等于之后的均衡回报；required premium 本身不能证明资产便宜。

## Question 5 · MCQ

<p class="source-note">Source: Local 原版书 / Core-Asset Allocation-CME_ORIG.md / No.2021052701000005-5 — adapted。</p>

<CfaQuestion id="2021052701000005-5" answer="A">

<template #prompt>

Based on Bader’s expectations for current account secular trends as shown in Exhibit 1, Bader should reallocate capital, all else being equal, from:

</template>

<template #a>

Country A to Country C.

</template>

<template #b>

Country B to Country A.

</template>

<template #c>

Country C to Country A.

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

A. Country A to Country C.

### Why?

A 的 current-account deficit 继续扩大、C surplus 上升；其他条件相同，C 的长期外部融资更有支撑。

### Distractor Analysis

B/C 把方向反转，忽略题问 secular trend。此为该模型的条件性倾向，非仅一项指标即可投资。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** PPP 以相对通胀提供长期锚，资本流动可主导短期；先检查报价方向。

国际政策制度接到货币预期，再进入外币资产的本币回报；资本突然撤出也会改变 emerging debt 和 liquidity 风险。

**Boundary：** 利差可能补偿贬值或风险；PPP 偏离可以持续，短期 capital flows 并非长期购买力证明。

<CfaQuestion id="2021052701000005-7" answer="B">

<template #prompt>

Based on Exhibit 1, which country’s central bank is **most likely** to buy domestic bonds near term to sterilize the impact of money flows on domestic liquidity?

</template>

<template #a>

Country A

</template>

<template #b>

Country B

</template>

<template #c>

Country C

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

B. Country B

### Why?

Hot money 从 B 流出，若央行为平抑外汇影响卖 FX 收回本币，domestic liquidity 下降；buy domestic bonds 可注入 liquidity 作 sterilization。

### Distractor Analysis

A/C 不具有该流出引发的 liquidity drain；C 流入对应的典型 sterilization 反而 sell domestic assets。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** PPP 以相对通胀提供长期锚，资本流动可主导短期；先检查报价方向。
