Which of the following statements about the use of investment factors in asset allocation is **most accurate**?

</template>

<template #a>

Investment factors generally have high correlations with each other

</template>

<template #b>

Investment factors generally have high correlations with the market

</template>

<template #c>

Investment factors are typically based on observed market premiums and anomalies

</template>

<template #analysis>

### Correct Answer

C. Investment factors are typically based on observed market premiums and anomalies

### Why?

Investment factors 常从 observed premiums/anomalies 构造，如 value/momentum。

### Distractor Analysis

A/B 的 always-high correlation 表述不合 factor diversification 的一般用途；实际相关性随时期/定义变。

### Knowledge Review

**Core：** 因子模型揭示跨资产共同风险；经济风险因素与可实施 investment-factor portfolios 要区分。

VCV 的 factor model 是风险估计工具，risk budgeting 用共同风险分配贡献；两者让资产标签下的暴露进入量化配置。

**Boundary：** 因子并非天然可交易，也不保证稳定低相关；实现工具会有剩余风险与成本。
