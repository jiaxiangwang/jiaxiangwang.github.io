<p>Definition of the Firm</p>
<p>Delta Asset Management is an independent investment management firm established in 2016. <p>Return Calculation</p>
<p>The return calculations are presented using money-weighted returns.</p>
<p>Benchmark</p>
<p>Sources of foreign exchange rates may be different between the composite and the benchmark.</p>
<p>Fees</p>

<p>A complete list and description of all composites is available upon request.</p>
<p>Verification</p>
<p>Delta Asset Management claims compliance with the Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS®) and has prepared and presented this report in compliance with the GIPS standards. Delta Asset Management has been independently verified for the period 01 July 2017 through 31 December 2023. The verification report is available upon request. A firm that claims compliance with the GIPS standards must establish policies and procedures for complying with all the applicable requirements of the GIPS standards. Verification provides assurance on whether the firm’s policies and procedures related to composite and pooled fund maintenance, as well as the calculation, presentation, and distribution of performance, have been designed in compliance with the GIPS standards and have been implemented on a firm-wide basis. Verification does not provide assurance on the accuracy of any specific performance report.</p>

<p>The following footnotes should be read in conjunction with the data in Exhibit 1:</p>
<p>Sonesta Advisers is a subsidiary of Sonesta LLC, which is registered with the Financial Conduct Authority.</p>
<p>The performance results represent fully discretionary, fee-paying, commingled, and separately managed accounts above the minimum investment of £1 million.</p>
<p>The name of this composite was changed from Core Bond Fund on 01 January 2017.</p>
<p>The composite creation date is 01 January 2010.</p>
<p>Trade date and accrual accounting are used. All performance calculations reflect reinvestment of dividends and accrued interest. Based on the system the portfolio resides on, either a monthly time-weighted rate of return formula is used, accounting for transactions on a daily weighted basis, or daily returns are geometrically linked to calculate monthly time-weighted returns. Beginning-of-period asset-weighted averages of portfolio returns constitute the monthly composite return. Annual composite results are derived by geometrically linking these monthly composite returns.</p>
<p>Gross performance results are presented before the deduction of investment advisory fees and custodial fees a client may have paid, but after all trading expenses.</p>
<p>Net performance figures were calculated by deducting the highest management fee each quarter from gross performance.</p>
<p>The benchmark is the FTSE (Financial Times Stock Exchange) Fixed Income Sustainable Finance index of maturities greater than one year.</p>
<p>Accounts in this composite include fixed income returns carved out of the Equal Weight Balanced Composite. The cash allocation methodology for the entire history presented was based on the adviser’s asset allocation policy at the beginning of the quarter or by the client’s specific asset allocation policy, if different.</p>
<p>The internal dispersion presented is an asset-weighted calculation of the annual standard deviation of accounts that were included in the composite for the entire year.</p>
